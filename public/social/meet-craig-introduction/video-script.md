# Meet Craig — 25-second video script

Status: prepared, not filmed or published.

## Spoken script

Hello, I’m Craig, the person behind Vegan Masala.

I grew up loving curry and taught myself to cook the dishes I couldn’t always afford to buy. When I became vegan, I started adapting those favourites without giving up the flavour that made me love them.

Vegan Masala is where I share what I’ve learned. Next, you’ll see more original cooking, practical techniques and stories from my kitchen.

Follow along — vegan food, cooked with love.

## Shot plan

1. **0–3 seconds:** Craig faces the camera in the Vegan Masala apron. On-screen text: “Hello, I’m Craig.”
2. **3–8 seconds:** Close-up of spices or opening a spice tin. On-screen text: “A lifelong love of curry.”
3. **8–14 seconds:** Stirring a curry or tempering spices. On-screen text: “Plant-based, without compromise.”
4. **14–20 seconds:** Craig plating or presenting the dish. On-screen text: “Recipes · techniques · stories.”
5. **20–25 seconds:** Direct-to-camera smile and branded end card. On-screen text: “Follow Vegan Masala.”

## Production rules

- Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920.
- Begin on a bright portrait frame, never black.
- Record near a window and switch off harsh overhead lights.
- Keep the kitchen real and tidy rather than looking like a studio set.
- Use Rajdhani Bold for all on-screen text.
- Use gold text and borders, restrained red accents and the visible mandala background on the end card.
- Include burned-in captions because many viewers watch without sound.
- Keep the logo clear but secondary to Craig’s face.
- End with “Follow Vegan Masala” and vegan-masala.com/about.
