# Meet Craig — photograph brief

The campaign can be completed from one short kitchen shoot. Supply the original, uncropped, full-resolution files rather than screenshots or social-media downloads.

## Essential photographs

1. **Main portrait:** waist-up, looking into the camera, wearing the Vegan Masala apron.
2. **Portrait with space:** the same pose but standing to one side so text can sit beside you.
3. **Cooking action:** stirring a pan, adding spices or rolling bread; hands and action must be clearly visible.
4. **Finished dish:** holding or standing beside a real dish you cooked.

## Framing

- Take each setup vertically and horizontally.
- Leave space around your head, hands and apron; do not crop tightly in-camera.
- Keep the Vegan Masala logo on the apron visible and uncreased.
- Take several frames both looking at the camera and looking naturally at the food.

## Light and setting

- Face a window or stand at roughly 45 degrees to it.
- Avoid mixing daylight with yellow overhead lighting.
- Use a recognisable home kitchen, but remove packaging, cables and clutter.
- Include real tools and ingredients you genuinely use.
- A phone camera is sufficient: use the rear camera at its standard 1× setting, clean the lens and avoid portrait-mode blur around the apron logo.

## Expression and clothing

- Warm, confident and relaxed rather than formal.
- Wear the branded apron over a plain black, deep red, cream or neutral top.
- Avoid fine stripes, large unrelated logos and highly reflective glasses where possible.

## Delivery

Send the original image files directly. Do not add filters, text, borders or crops—the campaign artwork will apply the brand treatment consistently afterward.
