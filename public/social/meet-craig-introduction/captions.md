## Instagram

Hello — I’m Craig, the person behind Vegan Masala.

I’m originally from Wales and now live in Bristol. My love of Indian cooking began with family recipes, treasured takeaways and a Madhur Jaffrey cookbook discovered on my grandparents’ shelf.

After becoming vegetarian and later vegan, I began adapting the curries I loved using plant-based ingredients. I found that, with the right techniques and spices, none of the generosity or depth of flavour had to be lost.

Vegan Masala grew from that idea: helping vegans, vegetarians and curious home cooks make satisfying Indian food without relying on meat or dairy.

Until now, the recipes have taken centre stage. The next chapter will bring more of me into the kitchen, with original cooking videos, practical methods, ingredient guides and the stories behind the food.

Thank you for following, cooking and helping Vegan Masala grow. I’m delighted to finally introduce myself.

Vegan food, cooked with love.

— Craig

## Facebook

Hello — I’m Craig, the home cook and founder behind Vegan Masala.

My love of Indian cooking began in Wales with family recipes, special-occasion takeaways and a Madhur Jaffrey cookbook I found on my grandparents’ shelf. After becoming vegetarian and then vegan, I began adapting the dishes I loved so they kept all their warmth, spice and generosity without relying on meat or dairy.

Vegan Masala became a place to share those recipes with vegans, vegetarians and anyone curious about eating more plant-based food. The next phase will be more personal: original cooking videos, practical methods, ingredient guides and more stories from my own kitchen in Bristol.

Thank you for being part of it. Follow the Vegan Masala Facebook page and read my full story here:
