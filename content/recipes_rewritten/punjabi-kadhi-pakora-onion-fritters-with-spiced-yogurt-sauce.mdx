## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup chickpea flour
- 1 tablespoon rice flour
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain, optional)
- 1 tablespoon non-dairy yogurt
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1/2 cup heaping chopped broccoli (chopped small)
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 cup non-dairy yogurt (such as Kite Hill almond yogurt or Forager cashew yogurt)
- 3 tablespoons chickpea flour
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
- 1 cup water
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/8 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- 2 dried chilies (hot Indian or Thai chilis, or mild varieties like California red)
- 2 cloves garlic (finely chopped)
- 1/2 inch ginger (finely chopped)
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne (or Kashmiri chili powder)

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 410°F (210°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a bowl, mix 1/2 cup chickpea flour, 1 tablespoon rice flour, 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon garam masala, 1/4 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon baking powder, and 1/4 teaspoon carom seeds if using.
3. Stir in 1 tablespoon non-dairy yogurt and 1/4 cup water to make a thick batter. Fold in 2 teaspoons oil, 1/2 cup heaping chopped broccoli, and 1/2 cup chopped onion.
4. Scoop the batter into small mounds on the prepared tray. Bake for about 20 minutes, until the fritters are browned and set. Broil for 1 minute if you want a darker top, then let them cool slightly.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk 1 cup non-dairy yogurt, 3 tablespoons chickpea flour, 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon cayenne. Whisk in 1 cup water until smooth and set aside.
6. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds, then add 1/8 teaspoon fenugreek seeds and 2 dried chilies.
7. Add 2 cloves garlic and 1/2 inch ginger, both finely chopped, and cook until the garlic loses its raw smell and starts to turn golden at the edges.
8. Lower the heat to medium-low and pour in the yogurt mixture slowly, stirring as you go. Bring it to a boil, then simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until the kadhi lightly thickens.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Gently add the baked fritters to the kadhi, or keep them beside the pot if you want them to stay a little firmer.
10. For the chilli oil, heat 2 teaspoons oil in a small pan over low heat. Take it off the heat and stir in 1/2 teaspoon cayenne or Kashmiri chili powder, then drizzle over the kadhi before serving.

## Notes
- Bake the fritters until properly browned; that keeps them intact when they go into the kadhi. If they soften while waiting, give them a few minutes in a hot oven before serving.
- The kadhi thickens as it sits, especially after the chickpea flour cooks in. Keep a splash of hot water nearby and thin it only as needed.
- Use a plain unsweetened non-dairy yogurt with some tang. Very thin yogurt will need a little more simmering to take on body.
- If your yogurt is very tart, hold back a little cayenne at first and add more at the end. The sauce should taste balanced, not sharp.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for a day or two. Reheat the kadhi gently over low heat and add the fritters at the end so they do not turn soggy.
