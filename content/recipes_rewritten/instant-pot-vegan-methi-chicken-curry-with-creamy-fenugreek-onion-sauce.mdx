## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup dry soy curls
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)
- 1 cup plant milk
- 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté and heat 2 tbsp oil.
2. Add 2 bay leaves, 2 green cardamom pods, 4 cloves and 1 small cinnamon stick. Let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 1 green chilli, finely chopped. Cook for 30 to 45 seconds.
5. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, and cook until it breaks down and the masala turns pulpy.
6. Pour in 1 cup water, then stir in 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin and 1 tsp salt. Scrape the base so nothing catches.
7. Add 1 cup dry soy curls, 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt and 1 tsp sugar, if using. Mix well so the soy curls are coated in the masala.
8. Seal the pot and pressure cook for 15 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally.
9. Open the lid and stir in 1 cup plant milk. Simmer on sauté mode until the curry turns creamy and thickens slightly.
10. Add 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) and 1 tsp garam masala. Stir through, then finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
11. Taste and adjust salt before serving.

## Notes
- Take the onion a little further than soft; a pale golden base gives the curry more depth and keeps the sauce from tasting thin.
- Stir the yoghurt in before pressure cooking and keep the masala mixed well so it blends into the gravy instead of clinging in patches.
- If the curry looks loose after cooking, simmer it open for a few minutes. Soy curls soften as they sit, so stop as soon as the sauce has body.
- Kasuri methi is strongest after a short rest. If you have time, let the curry sit for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of water or plant milk so the soy curls stay tender.
