## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons red onion, finely minced
- 1 teaspoon green chili, finely minced
- ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons cilantro, finely chopped
- ⅓ cup plant-based milk or water
- 2 slices bread, sourdough, multigrain or whole wheat
- 2 tablespoons oil, for cooking
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- ¼ cup cilantro mint chutney

## Method
1. In a wide bowl, mix 2 tablespoons finely minced red onion, 1 teaspoon finely minced green chili, ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric, ½ teaspoon kosher salt, 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro, and ⅓ cup plant-based milk or water until evenly combined.
2. Dip 1 slice of bread into the mixture and let it soak briefly, then turn it over so both sides are coated. Lift it out and let the excess drip back into the bowl.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pan over medium heat. Place the soaked bread in the pan and spoon any onion-herb mixture left in the bowl over the top.
4. Cook for about 1 minute on the first side, until the underside is golden and crisp. Flip carefully and cook the second side for another minute, until evenly browned. Repeat with the second slice of bread.
5. Serve hot with 2 tablespoons ketchup and ¼ cup cilantro mint chutney.

## Notes
- Use bread that can hold the soaking mixture without falling apart; thicker slices work better than very soft sandwich bread. The toast should be crisp at the edges and soft in the middle, not wet.
- Keep the heat at medium so the onion cooks through and the bread browns before the spices darken. Add a little more oil only if the pan looks dry.
- Leftovers reheat best in a dry pan over low heat. A toaster oven also works, but the toast will be less even than from the pan.
- For a milder version, reduce the green chili or leave out the extra onion spooned on top. For more body, serve with chutney rather than more ketchup.
