## Ingredients
- 14 ounces firm or extra firm tofu (pressed for 15 minutes, then torn into 3/4" pieces)
- 1 tablespoon non-dairy yogurt
- 1 tablespoon ground coriander
- 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon fennel seed powder (or crushed fennel seeds)
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne (or Kashmiri chili powder for milder heat)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt (for tofu seasoning)
- 2 teaspoons oil (for frying tofu)
- 2 teaspoons oil (for sauce)
- 1/4 teaspoon fennel seeds
- 2 green cardamom pods (slightly crushed)
- 2-inch cinnamon stick
- 1 1/2 cups chopped onion
- 10 curry leaves (fresh, frozen, or dried)
- 1 dried red chili (cayenne or Kashmiri chili, or omit for less heat)
- 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste (or 4 cloves garlic and 1" ginger, minced)
- 1 cup chopped tomato
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (for sauce)
- 1 teaspoon of the reserved tofu spice mix
- 1 cup water (or adjust with coconut milk/non-dairy cream)
- 1 teaspoon lime juice, plus more to serve if needed, divided (1 teaspoon in the sauce)

## Method
1. Press the 14 ounces firm or extra firm tofu for 15 minutes, then tear it into 3/4" pieces. Toss with 1 tablespoon non-dairy yogurt until evenly coated.
2. In a bowl, mix 1 tablespoon ground coriander, 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper, 1/8 teaspoon fennel seed powder, 1/2 teaspoon cayenne, and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Reserve 1 teaspoon of the spice mix for the sauce, then coat the tofu with the rest.
3. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and fry for about 8 minutes, turning every few minutes, until the edges are golden. Lift it out and set aside.
4. In the same skillet, heat 2 teaspoons oil over medium. Add 1/4 teaspoon fennel seeds and cook for about 15 seconds, then add 2 green cardamom pods and a 2-inch cinnamon stick. Stir in 1 1/2 cups chopped onion with a pinch of salt and cook for 2 minutes.
5. Add 10 curry leaves and 1 dried red chili. Cook the onion, stirring often and splashing in a little water if needed, until it turns golden, about 5 to 7 minutes.
6. Stir in 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste, then add 1 cup chopped tomato and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cook until the tomato breaks down and looks jammy, about 3 to 5 minutes, pressing down any larger pieces.
7. Add the reserved 1 teaspoon of the spice mix, then pour in 1 cup water and 1 teaspoon lime juice. Bring the sauce to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 2 minutes.
8. Fold in the fried tofu, cover, and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes so the tofu takes on the sauce. Taste and add more lime juice if needed, then finish with cilantro.
9. Serve hot with rice, dosa, or naan.

## Notes
- If you want a softer sauce, stir in coconut cream or more non-dairy yogurt at the end, but add it off the heat so it does not split.
- Baked tofu works well too: toss it with the yogurt and spice mix, then bake at 400°F (205°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until lightly crisp.
- The curry keeps well for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently with a splash of water, because the sauce thickens as it sits.
- The heat comes from the black pepper and cayenne as much as the chili, so adjust those first if you want a milder curry.
