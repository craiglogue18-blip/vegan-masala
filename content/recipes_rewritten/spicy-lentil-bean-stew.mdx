## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1½ cups chopped onion
- ½ teaspoon salt, divided
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 black cardamom pod, partially opened, optional
- ¼ teaspoon crushed fennel seeds
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder (or paprika)
- ½ teaspoon ground cardamom
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon (scant measure)
- 1/8 teaspoon ground clove
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon garam masala (or curry powder)
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste (or 5 cloves garlic minced and half an inch ginger minced)
- 2 tablespoons water or stock, divided
- 1 cup chopped tomato (or ½ cup tomato purée or Passata)
- 15 oz can full-fat coconut milk
- 1½ cups water or stock
- ¼ to ⅓ cup split red lentils
- 15 oz can white beans
- ½ cup frozen spinach, thawed, or 2 oz fresh chopped spinach
- cilantro, lemon juice, red pepper flakes, for garnish","non-dairy cream or yogurt, for garnish, optional"],

## Method
1. Mix 1 teaspoon black pepper, 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, ½ teaspoon ground cardamom, ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon, 1/8 teaspoon ground clove, 2 teaspoons ground coriander, and 1 teaspoon garam masala in a small bowl.
2. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add 1½ cups chopped onion with a pinch of the ½ teaspoon salt and cook for about 3 minutes, until translucent.
3. Add 1 bay leaf, 1 black cardamom pod if using, and ¼ teaspoon crushed fennel seeds. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes more, adding 2 tablespoons water or stock if the pan starts to catch and the onions need time to soften.
4. When the onions are lightly golden, add three-quarters of the spice blend with 2 tablespoons water or stock. Stir for 10 to 15 seconds, then add 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste and 1 cup chopped tomato. Cook for 3 to 5 minutes, until the tomatoes break down and the masala looks thick and glossy.
5. Pour in 15 oz full-fat coconut milk, 1½ cups water or stock, the remaining salt, ¼ to ⅓ cup split red lentils, and 15 oz white beans. Stir well, partly cover, and simmer for 12 to 15 minutes, until the lentils are soft and the stew has a creamy body.
6. Stir in ½ cup thawed frozen spinach, or 2 oz fresh chopped spinach, and cook for 1 minute, just until heated through. Taste and add lemon juice if the stew needs brightness.
7. Turn off the heat and scatter the remaining spice blend over the top. Finish with cilantro, red pepper flakes, and a little non-dairy cream or yogurt if you want a softer finish.

## Notes
- The stew thickens as it rests because the lentils keep absorbing liquid. Add a splash of water or stock when reheating if you want a looser bowl.
- For a deeper chili note, use the full amount of Kashmiri chili powder and finish with red pepper flakes. For a milder pan, use paprika and keep the final spice sprinkle light.
- If you use tomato purée instead of chopped tomato, cook it down until the raw sharpness disappears before adding the coconut milk.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat so the coconut milk stays smooth and the beans hold their shape.
