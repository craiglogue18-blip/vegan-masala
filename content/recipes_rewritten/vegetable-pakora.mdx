## Ingredients
- 750 ml neutral oil, for deep-frying
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 cup spinach, chopped
- 1 large potato, thinly sliced
- 1 cup cauliflower florets
- 1 cup gram flour (besan)
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water

## Method
1. Heat 750 ml neutral oil in a deep pan over medium heat until steady and ready for frying.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine 1 cup gram flour (besan), 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon garam masala, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 tsp salt.
3. Add 1 large onion, thinly sliced, 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped, 1 cup spinach, chopped, 1 large potato, thinly sliced, and 1 cup cauliflower florets.
4. Pour in 1 cup water a little at a time, mixing until the vegetables are coated in a thick batter that clings to them without running off.
5. Drop spoonfuls of the batter-coated vegetables into the hot oil, keeping each portion small so the inside cooks through before the outside darkens.
6. Fry for 5-7 minutes, turning as needed, until the pakora are deep golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately.

## Notes
- Keep the batter thick. If it loosens too much, the pakora will drink oil instead of frying crisp.
- The onion and potato should be sliced thin enough to cook through in the frying time. Small cauliflower florets help the centres soften before the crust overcolours.
- Fry in steady oil; if the heat drops, the pakora will turn heavy and greasy.
- These are best eaten as soon as they drain. To reheat, use a hot oven or air fryer until the crust firms up again.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days in an airtight container, though the surface will soften overnight.
