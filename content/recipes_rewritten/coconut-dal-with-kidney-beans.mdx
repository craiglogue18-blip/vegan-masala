## Ingredients
- 75g/2½oz vegan butter, divided
- 1 very large onion, finely chopped, or 200g/7oz frozen diced onion
- 2 tbsp garlic and ginger paste, or 1 tbsp garlic paste and 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 2 tbsp medium curry powder
- pinch of chilli or cayenne pepper (optional)
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 400g tin full-fat coconut milk
- 400g tin brown lentils, rinsed and drained
- 400g tin kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- salt and black pepper
- fresh coriander, to garnish (optional)

## Method
1. Melt 37.5g of the vegan butter in a frying pan over low-medium heat. Add the onion, garlic and ginger paste, curry powder and the chilli or cayenne, if using. Cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring often, until the onion is soft and the spices smell cooked.
2. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and full-fat coconut milk, then season with a pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 8-10 minutes, until the tomatoes have lost their raw edge and the sauce has started to thicken.
3. Add the brown lentils, kidney beans and the remaining 37.5g vegan butter. Simmer gently for about 20-30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the dal is creamy and the beans are coated in the sauce.
4. Take the pan off the heat and leave it to sit for a few minutes. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper, then loosen with a splash of water if needed. Finish with fresh coriander, if using.

## Notes
- The dal thickens as it cools, so save a little hot water for loosening it when reheating or serving later.
- Reheat gently on the hob over low heat. Hard boiling can split the coconut milk and dull the texture.
- If you want a softer, more spoonable finish, mash a small scoop of the lentils against the side of the pan near the end of cooking.
- This keeps well in the fridge for up to 4 days and usually settles into a deeper masala flavour by the next day.
