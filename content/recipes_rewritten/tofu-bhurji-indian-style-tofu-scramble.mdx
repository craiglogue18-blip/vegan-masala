## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/2 cup onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon green chilli, chopped
- 1/2 cup bell pepper, chopped
- 1/4 cup tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds, crushed (optional)
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin and coriander powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala (or cinnamon powder)
- 400 grams tofu, drained and pressed, crumbled
- salt to taste
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

## Method
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 teaspoon cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
2. Add 1/2 cup chopped onion, 1 tablespoon minced ginger-garlic, and 1 tablespoon chopped green chilli. Cook until the onion softens and loses its raw smell.
3. Stir in 1/2 cup chopped bell pepper and cook for a minute or two, keeping a little bite in the pepper.
4. Add 1/4 cup chopped tomatoes, 1/2 teaspoon crushed fennel seeds if using, 1 teaspoon red chilli powder, 1 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon ground cumin and coriander powder, and 1/2 teaspoon garam masala or cinnamon powder. Cook until the tomatoes break down and the masala looks lightly cooked.
5. Add 400 grams crumbled tofu and salt to taste. Mix well so the spices coat the tofu evenly.
6. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring gently, until the tofu is heated through and the mixture looks dry but not brittle.
7. Finish with 1 tablespoon lemon juice and fold through. Add chopped cilantro if you like.

## Notes
- Drain and press the tofu well so it crumbles cleanly and does not turn watery in the pan.
- If you want a softer bhurji, use a little extra tomato and cook it down before adding the tofu.
- Leftovers keep well for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a pan over low heat with a spoonful of water if needed.
- This works well with roti, toast, or pav, and it also makes a good filling for wraps.
