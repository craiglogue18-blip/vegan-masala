## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 inch cinnamon stick
- 1 cup chopped red onion
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder (or paprika)
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste (or 4 cloves garlic and 1/2 inch ginger, minced)
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste (or 3 tablespoons tomato puree)
- 7 to 8 ounces frozen spinach
- 1 cup water (or non-dairy milk)
- 1/4 cup non-dairy yogurt (or non-dairy cream)
- 15 ounce can butter beans, drained (or other white beans, like cannellini or chickpeas)
- 1 teaspoon oil, for tempering
- 1 whole red chili, broken in half
- 1 clove garlic, thinly sliced
- pepper flakes, for garnish (optional)","vegan parmesan, for garnish (optional)"

## Method
1. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds and cook until they darken and smell aromatic. Add 2 bay leaves and 1 inch cinnamon stick, stir for 10 seconds, then add 2 teaspoons ground coriander, 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala, 1/2 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves, and 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. If the spices begin to catch, lower the heat and add 1 cup chopped red onion with 1/4 teaspoon salt.
2. Cook the onion over medium heat, stirring and adding small splashes of water as needed, until it softens and takes on a little colour, 6 to 8 minutes. Stir in 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste and 1 tablespoon tomato paste, then add a splash of water and cook until the masala looks glossy and the tomato paste loses its raw edge. Add the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt, 7 to 8 ounces frozen spinach, 1 cup water, and 1/4 cup non-dairy yogurt. Fold in 15 ounce drained butter beans, cover, and simmer for 6 to 8 minutes.
3. Taste and adjust salt, heat, and thickness. If you want a looser saag, add a little more water or non-dairy milk; if you want it thicker, cook uncovered for 1 to 2 minutes more. Finish with pepper flakes and vegan parmesan, if using.
4. For the tempering, heat 1 teaspoon oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add 1 whole red chili, broken in half, and cook until it darkens slightly. Add 1 clove garlic and cook until golden at the edges and fragrant, then remove from the heat at once.
5. Spoon the garlic chilli oil over the saag butter beans just before serving.

## Notes
- The saag should hold a creamy, spoonable texture with the beans still intact. If the spinach looks too loose at first, give it a minute uncovered before serving; it thickens as it sits.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stove with a splash of water or non-dairy milk, since the beans and spinach will tighten up overnight.
- For a sharper finish, use the full amount of black pepper and the hotter end of the red chili. For a milder bowl, keep the Kashmiri chili powder low and go easy on the tempering chili.
