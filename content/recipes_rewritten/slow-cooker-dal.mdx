## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- thumb of fresh root ginger, finely grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 300g yellow split peas
- 200g chopped tomatoes (from a tin or fresh)
- 10 freeze-dried curry leaves
- 700ml hot vegetable stock
- 1 hot green finger chilli, thinly sliced, divided
- 2 garlic cloves, one crushed and one thinly sliced
- salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- lemon wedges, to serve

## Method
1. Put the 300g yellow split peas, 1 chopped onion, 200g chopped tomatoes, thumb of grated ginger, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground turmeric, 1 crushed garlic clove, 10 freeze-dried curry leaves, 700ml hot vegetable stock and most of the 1 thinly sliced green chilli into the slow cooker. Stir well so the spices are evenly mixed through.
2. Cook on high for 4 hours, or until the split peas are fully tender and the dal has broken down to a thick, spoonable consistency. Stir once or twice if you can.
3. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper. If the dal looks too loose, cook uncovered for a few minutes; if it is too thick, loosen it with a splash of hot water.
4. Heat 2 tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil in a small saucepan until hot. Add the remaining 1 thinly sliced garlic clove and fry until golden, then pour it straight over the dal.
5. Finish with the remaining sliced green chilli and serve at once with lemon wedges for squeezing.

## Notes
- The dal thickens as it sits, so keep back a little hot water if you want a looser texture for serving or reheating.
- For a smoother bowl, blend part of the cooked dal with a spoon or stick blender. Leave it as is for a more rustic texture.
- It freezes well in portions for up to 2 months. Thaw fully, then reheat gently and stir so the split peas loosen evenly.
- If you want a milder dal, use less of the chilli in the cooker and save the rest for the finish. That keeps the heat brighter and easier to control.
- A little fresh coriander works well at the table, but the dal only needs rice, lemon and something crisp alongside.
