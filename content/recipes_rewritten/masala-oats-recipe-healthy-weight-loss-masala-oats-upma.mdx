## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegan ghee or neutral oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 pods cardamom
- ½ tsp cloves
- 1 inch cinnamon stick
- ½ onion (finely chopped)
- 2 green chillies (slit)
- ½ carrot (chopped)
- 5 green beans (chopped)
- 2 tbsp peas
- ½ tomato (chopped)
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp pav bhaji masala
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 cup palak (chopped)
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves (chopped)

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp vegan ghee or neutral oil in a large kadai over medium heat. Add 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 4 pods cardamom, ½ tsp cloves and 1 inch cinnamon stick, and let them sizzle until aromatic.
2. Add ½ onion, finely chopped, and 2 green chillies, slit. Cook until the onion softens and turns translucent.
3. Add ½ carrot, 5 green beans and 2 tbsp peas. Stir for a minute, then add ½ tomato, chopped, and cook until it begins to break down.
4. Add ½ tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp pav bhaji masala and ½ tsp salt. Stir well so the vegetables are coated in the masala.
5. Add 1 cup chopped palak and cook for 2 minutes, just until it wilts into the masala.
6. Pour in 3 cups water and bring the mixture to a boil.
7. Stir in 1 cup rolled oats, then lower the heat and mix well so the oats do not clump.
8. Cover and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring once or twice and adding a little more water if the oats tighten too much, until soft and porridge-like.
9. Turn off the heat and mix in 2 tsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves. Serve hot.
10. notes
11. The oats will thicken as they sit, so loosen them with a splash of hot water when reheating. The texture should stay spoonable, not dry.
12. Keep the spice level steady with the green chillies and red chilli powder, or reduce both if you want a milder bowl.
13. Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat gently on the stove rather than in a very hot pan, so the oats do not catch at the bottom.

## Notes
- The oats will thicken as they sit, so loosen them with a splash of hot water when reheating. The texture should stay spoonable, not dry.
- Keep the spice level steady with the green chillies and red chilli powder, or reduce both if you want a milder bowl.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat gently on the stove rather than in a very hot pan, so the oats do not catch at the bottom.
