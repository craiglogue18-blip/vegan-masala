## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil, divided
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- lemon wedge (optional)

## Method
1. Pat the 200 g firm tofu cubes dry with a clean towel. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a pan and fry the tofu until lightly golden on the edges. Lift it out and set it aside.
2. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to the same pan. Stir in 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until it turns deep golden and the edges start to soften and brown.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for about 1 minute, until the raw smell fades.
5. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook them down until they break apart and the masala starts to pull away from the sides of the pan.
6. Add 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, and 1 tsp salt. Stir well and cook for a minute so the spices lose their raw edge.
7. Add 3 medium potatoes, cubed, and 1 cup water. Cover and simmer until the potatoes are tender and the gravy has thickened.
8. Fold in the fried tofu and simmer gently for a few minutes so it takes on the masala without breaking apart.
9. Finish with 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp sugar, if using. Taste and adjust the salt only if needed.
10. Stir through 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve with a squeeze of lemon, if you like.

## Notes
- Take the onion to a real golden colour; that is where the curry gets its base. Pale onion will leave the masala tasting thin.
- Fry the tofu before adding it back to the pan. It helps the cubes stay firm in the gravy instead of soaking up too much liquid and collapsing.
- Let the tomatoes cook fully before adding the potatoes. When the masala is properly cooked, it should smell rounded and the oil should show at the edges.
- If the pan looks dry before the potatoes are tender, add a small splash of water rather than letting the spices catch.
- The potatoes should be soft at the centre but still hold their shape. If needed, keep the lid on a little longer and test with a knife.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the hob with a splash of water to loosen the gravy again.
