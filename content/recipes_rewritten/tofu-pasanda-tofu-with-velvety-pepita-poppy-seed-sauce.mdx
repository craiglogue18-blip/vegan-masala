## Ingredients
- 14 ounces extra-firm tofu
- 1 teaspoon oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds
- 1 whole clove
- 1 cup chopped red onion
- 3/4 teaspoon salt, divided
- 1 inch ginger, finely chopped (or 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3 tablespoons pumpkin seeds (or sunflower seeds, almonds, or cashews)
- 1 teaspoon white or black poppy seeds
- 1 large tomato, chopped (about 1 cup)
- 1 cup coconut milk (or other non-dairy milk)
- 1/2 cup or more water
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar (or maple syrup)
- cilantro and non-dairy cream for garnish

## Method
1. If needed, press the 14 ounces extra-firm tofu for at least 15 minutes, then tear it into bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat 1/2 teaspoon of the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the tofu with 1 teaspoon garam masala and 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, and cook until the edges turn lightly golden, 4 to 6 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
3. In the same skillet, heat the remaining 1/2 teaspoon oil over medium heat. Add 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon coriander seeds, 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds, and 1 whole clove. Cook for a few seconds, just until fragrant.
4. Add 1 cup chopped red onion and 1/4 teaspoon of the salt. Cook, stirring often, until the onion softens and turns golden at the edges, 7 to 9 minutes.
5. Stir in 1 inch finely chopped ginger and 4 cloves finely chopped garlic. Cook for 1 minute, then add 3 tablespoons pumpkin seeds, 1 teaspoon poppy seeds, 1 large chopped tomato, and the remaining salt. Cook until the tomato breaks down and the masala turns thick and pasty, about 5 minutes.
6. Take the pan off the heat and let it cool for a minute. Transfer the mixture to a blender, add 1 cup coconut milk, and blend until very smooth, pausing if needed so the seeds fully break down.
7. Return the sauce to the skillet and set it over medium heat. Add the tofu, 1/2 cup or more water, 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika, 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne, and 1/2 teaspoon sugar. Stir gently and simmer over medium-low heat until the sauce thickens and clings to the tofu, 15 to 20 minutes.
8. Turn off the heat and finish with cilantro and a little non-dairy cream, if using.

## Notes
- Blend the masala until it is fully smooth; any graininess usually comes from under-blended seeds.
- The sauce thickens as it cools. When reheating, add a splash of water and warm it slowly so the tofu stays tender.
- Taste at the end and adjust the salt, cayenne, or sugar if the tomato is sharper than you want.
- Leftovers keep well in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat over low heat or in short microwave bursts, stirring once or twice.
