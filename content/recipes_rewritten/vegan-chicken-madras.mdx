## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 pack Love Seitan Coarse Shredded Seitan Chyck’n (or similar)
- 5 cardamom pods
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, chopped into small pieces
- 1 tbsp garlic puree
- 2 tbsp tomato puree
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli
- 2 tbsp madras curry powder
- 400ml water
- small handful fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add 1 pack Love Seitan Coarse Shredded Seitan Chyck’n (or similar) and fry until the edges turn golden and crisp. Lift it out and set aside.
2. In the same pan, add 5 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick and 1 tsp cumin seeds. Stir for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, chopped into small pieces, and cook for 5 minutes until softened and starting to colour. Stir in 1 tbsp garlic puree and cook for 1 minute more.
4. Add 2 tbsp tomato puree, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp chilli and 2 tbsp madras curry powder. Stir well and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, letting the masala darken slightly and the tomato lose its raw edge.
5. Pour in 400ml water and bring the sauce to a steady simmer. Return the seitan to the pan, cover and cook for 20 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the sauce has thickened and the seitan is well coated.
6. Stir through a small handful of chopped fresh coriander just before serving.

## Notes
- If you want a looser sauce, add a splash more water near the end of simmering. For a thicker masala, cook uncovered for the last 5 minutes.
- Taste before serving and adjust salt or chilli only after the sauce has reduced; the heat reads stronger once the masala thickens.
- Leftovers keep well for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently on the hob with a little water if the sauce has tightened.
- The seitan keeps its bite after reheating, so this curry suits next-day rice or flatbread lunches as well as the first meal.
