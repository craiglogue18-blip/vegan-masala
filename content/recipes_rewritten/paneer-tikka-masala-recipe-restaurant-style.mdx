## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 100 ml oat cream
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)

## Method
1. In a bowl, mix 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1/2 tsp salt.
2. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, and 1 diced bell pepper. Coat well and marinate for 1 hour.
3. Bake the tofu and pepper at 200°C for 15 to 20 minutes, or pan-fry until the edges are lightly charred.
4. Blend 1 large finely chopped onion with 2 medium chopped tomatoes to a smooth purée.
5. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and cook the onion-tomato purée until it darkens and the raw smell is gone.
6. Add 4 cloves garlic, minced, and cook for a few seconds, stirring so it does not catch.
7. Stir in 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves, 2 tbsp fresh coriander and the remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Cook for 1 minute.
8. Pour in 1 cup water and simmer until the gravy thickens and the tomato taste softens.
9. Add 100 ml oat cream and 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed, then fold in the baked tofu and pepper.
10. Simmer gently for 2 to 3 minutes. Taste and add 1 tsp sugar, if using, to round out the sauce.
11. Serve hot, with the masala spooned over the tofu and chickpeas.
12. Scatter over a little extra fresh coriander before serving, if you like.

## Notes
- Cook the masala until the oil starts to separate at the edges; that is where the gravy gets its depth and a steadier tomato base.
- Keep the heat low when the oat cream goes in. A gentle simmer is enough and helps the sauce stay smooth.
- If the sauce thickens after standing, loosen it with a splash of water when reheating.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. The spices settle overnight, so the curry often tastes even better the next day.
- For a smokier finish, let the tofu and pepper take on a little more colour in the oven or pan before adding them to the sauce.
