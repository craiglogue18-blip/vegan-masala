## Ingredients
- 1 medium head cauliflower, chopped into florets
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 recipe Tikka Masala Sauce
- 1/4 cup non-dairy cream, such as blended cashews, soy, or coconut

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 400°F (205°C). In a large bowl, toss 1 medium head cauliflower florets with 2 tsp oil and 1 tsp lemon juice until coated.
2. In a small bowl, mix 1/2 tsp ground cumin, 1/2 tsp ground coriander, 1/2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and 1/2 tsp salt. Add the spice mix to the cauliflower and toss well.
3. Spread the cauliflower on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Roast for 25 minutes, until the edges are browned and the florets are tender but still hold their shape.
4. Warm 1 recipe Tikka Masala Sauce in a saucepan over medium heat or in an Instant Pot on sauté mode.
5. Add the roasted cauliflower to the sauce and stir gently. For a richer finish, stir in 1/4 cup non-dairy cream. If you want to add chickpeas, fold them in now as well.
6. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the cauliflower is well coated and the sauce is hot. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
7. Finish with fenugreek leaves or cilantro, then serve hot.

## Notes
- Roasting the cauliflower first keeps the florets from turning soft in the sauce. You want browned edges and a firm bite before it goes into the pan.
- The sauce thickens as it sits. If it feels too loose, simmer it a little longer before serving; if it gets too thick on reheating, loosen it with a splash of water.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat so the cauliflower stays intact and the cream does not split.
- Coconut cream gives a fuller finish, while cashew or soy cream stays a little lighter. Use whichever suits the sauce you are serving with.
