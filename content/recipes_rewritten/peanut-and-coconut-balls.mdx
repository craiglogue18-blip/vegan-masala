## Ingredients
- 250g/9oz roasted unsalted peanuts
- 100g/3½oz dates, roughly chopped
- 100g/3½oz chia seeds, divided
- 50g/1¾oz linseeds (also known as flaxseeds), divided
- 50g/1¾oz desiccated coconut
- 2 tbsp warm water, plus more if needed

## Method
1. Put 250g/9oz roasted unsalted peanuts, 100g/3½oz roughly chopped dates, 50g/1¾oz chia seeds and 25g/¾oz linseeds into a food processor. Blitz for 2–3 minutes, scraping down the sides once or twice, until the mixture looks coarse, sticky and starts to clump.
2. Add 2 tbsp warm water and blend for 1 minute more, until the mixture holds together when pressed. If it still feels dry and crumbly, add a little more warm water, 1 teaspoon at a time.
3. Tip the mixture onto a board and divide it into 20 equal portions. Roll each portion firmly between your palms into a tight ball.
4. Mix 50g/1¾oz desiccated coconut with the remaining 50g/1¾oz chia seeds and 25g/¾oz linseeds in a shallow bowl. Roll each ball through the coating until well covered.

## Notes
- The mixture should be dense and chewy, not wet or oily. If it feels greasy, the peanuts may have been ground too far; stop as soon as it begins to clump.
- If the dates are dry, soak them in hot water for 5 minutes, then drain very well before using.
- Roll the balls firmly so they hold together after chilling. If the mixture softens while you work, chill it for 10 minutes and carry on.
- These keep well in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week. They also freeze well; thaw in the fridge before serving.
- For a neater coating, press the balls lightly into the coconut and seed mixture instead of tossing them around in it.
