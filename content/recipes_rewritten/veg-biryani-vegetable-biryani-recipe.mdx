## Ingredients
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 2 tablespoons oil (coconut or vegetable)
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, bell peppers, etc.)
- 2 cups water
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnishing

## Method
1. Rinse 1 cup basmati rice well until the water runs clear, then soak it in fresh water for 15 minutes. Drain it thoroughly and set aside.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pot, pressure cooker, or Instant Pot over medium heat. Add 1 large thinly sliced onion and cook until it turns deep golden at the edges.
3. Add 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds and 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds. When they crackle, stir in 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon garam masala. Stir for about 30 seconds so the spices bloom in the hot oil.
5. Add 2 medium chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down and the masala looks thick and glossy.
6. Add 1 cup mixed vegetables and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, just until they are coated in the masala and slightly softened.
7. Stir in the drained rice, then add 2 cups water and salt to taste. Mix gently so the grains do not break.
8. If using an Instant Pot, cook on high pressure for 5 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes. In a pressure cooker, cook for 2 whistles, then rest off the heat. In a pot, cover and cook on low until the rice is done and the water is absorbed.
9. Rest the biryani for 5 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork. Finish with fresh coriander leaves.

## Notes
- Soaking helps the basmati cook evenly and stay separate, so do not skip that step.
- The onion should be properly golden before the tomatoes go in; pale onion gives the biryani a flatter taste.
- If your vegetables soften quickly, keep the cooking time brief after adding them so they hold their shape.
- For a drier biryani, reduce the water slightly next time; for softer rice, add a small splash more water.
- Leftovers keep well for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat with a spoonful of water over low heat so the rice loosens without drying out.
