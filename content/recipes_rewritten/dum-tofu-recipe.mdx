## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 4 cloves
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 100 ml oat cream
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- crushed black peppercorn, for garnish
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- mint leaves, for garnish

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add 4 cloves, 2 green cardamom pods and 1 small cinnamon stick. Let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until deep golden, stirring often so it does not catch or burn.
4. Stir in 1 inch ginger, grated, 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until the raw smell settles.
5. Add 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder and 1 tsp salt. Stir well so the spices coat the onion base and cook for 20 to 30 seconds.
6. Add 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt and stir until the masala turns smooth and lightly glossy.
7. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, and turn the pieces gently so they stay intact.
8. Pour in 100 ml oat cream and 1 cup water. Stir and bring the gravy to a gentle simmer.
9. Season with 1/2 tsp black pepper and scatter in crushed black peppercorn to taste.
10. Cover the pan with a lid and foil if needed, then lower the heat and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until the tofu has taken on the masala and the gravy has reduced slightly.
11. Lift the lid and stir in 1 red chilli, finely chopped, and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
12. Finish with mint leaves, stir once more and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take the onion on to a deep golden stage; that is where the gravy gets its body and the yoghurt stays balanced.
- Keep the heat gentle once the yoghurt and oat cream go in. A hard boil can split the sauce and blunt the masala.
- Firm tofu works best here. Pressing it for 10 to 15 minutes will help it hold shape and take on more of the gravy.
- If the gravy thickens too much, loosen it with a little hot water. If it feels thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes at the end.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat over low heat with a splash of water.
- The flavour settles as it sits, so the curry is often even better after a short rest before serving.
