## Ingredients
- 14 ounces firm or extra firm tofu, pressed for at least 15 minutes and torn into bite-sized pieces
- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons oil, divided
- 2 small dried red chilies
- 10 curry leaves, fresh, frozen, or dried
- 3/4 cup sliced onion
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 green chili, slit
- 2 to 3 tablespoons sliced green bell pepper
- 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 1/2 cup water mixed with 1 teaspoon cornstarch, for slurry, divided from the cornstarch above if preferred for mixing off the pan?

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (205°C).
2. Put the pressed tofu in a bowl and add 1 teaspoon oil. Toss gently so the surface is lightly coated.
3. In a small bowl, mix 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/8 teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and 2 tablespoons cornstarch. Sprinkle this over the tofu and toss until the pieces are evenly coated.
4. Spread the tofu on a parchment-lined baking sheet in a single layer. Bake for 22 to 28 minutes, until crisp at the edges. If you prefer the stovetop, heat 1 teaspoon of the 2 teaspoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the tofu for about 5 to 6 minutes total, turning until browned on several sides. Transfer to a plate.
5. Heat the remaining 1 teaspoon oil in the same skillet over medium-high heat. Add 2 small dried red chilies and 10 curry leaves, and stir for a few seconds until fragrant.
6. Add 3/4 cup sliced onion and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring often, until the onions soften and the edges take on colour. Add a splash of water if the pan starts catching.
7. Stir in 1 slit green chili and 2 to 3 tablespoons sliced green bell pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
8. Add 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste, 1 1/2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, and 2 tablespoons ketchup. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring well so the masala cooks through.
9. Mix 1 teaspoon cornstarch with 1/2 cup water. Stir this slurry into the pan and simmer until the sauce looks glossy and lightly thickened.
10. Add the tofu and toss to coat in the masala. Turn off the heat and finish with green onions, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime juice.

## Notes
- Baking gives the most even crisp edge, while pan-frying brings a darker, more blistered surface. Use whichever suits the day.
- Let the sauce thicken before adding the tofu. Once the tofu goes in, it only needs a brief toss so it stays crisp at the edges.
- The sauce should cling to the tofu in a thin layer rather than sit in a pool. If it tightens too much, loosen it with a spoonful of water.
- For more heat, add another green chili or a little extra black pepper. More garam masala will change the balance of the dish quickly.
- Leftovers keep well for 2 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet over medium heat, not the microwave, so the coating firms up again.
