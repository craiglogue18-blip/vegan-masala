## Ingredients
- 1 cup moong dal, rinsed
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Add the 1 cup rinsed moong dal, 1 large finely chopped onion, 2 medium chopped tomatoes, 1 inch grated ginger, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp salt, and 1 cup water to the pressure cooker.
2. Seal and cook on High Pressure for 20 minutes.
3. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then release any remaining pressure carefully.
4. Open the lid and stir well, breaking up some of the dal so the texture turns soft and even.
5. Stir in 1 tsp sugar and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
6. Taste and adjust with a little more salt or lemon if needed.
7. Finish with 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander and serve hot.

## Notes
- Whole moong dal should be tender but still hold a little shape, with enough body to coat a spoon. If it is thicker than you like, loosen it with hot water after cooking.
- If the tomatoes taste sharp, let the dal sit for a few minutes off the heat before serving; the masala settles and rounds out.
- For a looser dal, add hot water a little at a time after pressure cooking. For a thicker bowl, simmer uncovered for a few minutes.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat cleanly on the stove with a splash of water.
