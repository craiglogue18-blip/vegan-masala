## Ingredients
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Boil 3 medium potatoes, cubed, until just tender, then drain well and peel if needed.
2. Heat 2 tbsp vegetable oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp mustard seeds and let them crackle.
3. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, then tip in the boiled potatoes and 1 tsp salt.
4. Sprinkle over 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp chilli powder, 2 tsp coriander powder and 1 tsp garam masala.
5. Stir gently so the potatoes are evenly coated, then cook for 5 to 7 minutes. Let the masala dry onto the potatoes and take on a light toasted edge. If the pan looks dry too quickly, add a small splash of water.
6. Turn off the heat and finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Serve warm.

## Notes
- Use potatoes that hold their shape, so the cubes stay intact while the spices cling to the surface.
- If the potatoes are freshly boiled and very soft, let them cool for a few minutes before tossing them through the masala.
- A little water is only there to stop the spices from catching; too much will make the dish wet rather than dry.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a pan over low heat so the potatoes regain a little edge.
- The lemon is best added at the end, once the pan is off the heat, so the finish stays bright.
