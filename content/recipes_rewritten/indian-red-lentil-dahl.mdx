## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tin coconut milk
- coconut sugar, optional
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp coconut oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden.
2. Stir in 3 garlic cloves, minced, and 1-inch piece of ginger, grated. Cook for 30 seconds, just until the raw smell fades.
3. Add 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garam masala, and 1 tsp ground cumin. Stir for about 1 minute so the spices bloom in the oil.
4. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down into a thick masala and the oil begins to show at the edges.
5. Stir in 1 cup red lentils, rinsed, and 4 cups vegetable broth. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then.
6. Add 1 tin coconut milk and 1 tsp salt. Simmer until the lentils are very soft and the dal has a creamy, spoonable texture.
7. Taste and adjust with coconut sugar, if needed, then stir in 1 tbsp lemon juice. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take time with the onion and tomato base; that is what gives the dal depth and keeps the spice mix from tasting thin.
- Red lentils cook fast and break down easily, so keep the heat low enough that the dal moves in slow bubbles, not a hard boil.
- If the dal thickens while sitting, loosen it with a little hot water or broth before serving or reheating.
- The lemon juice is best added at the end so the coconut milk stays smooth and the dal tastes rounded rather than sharp.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
