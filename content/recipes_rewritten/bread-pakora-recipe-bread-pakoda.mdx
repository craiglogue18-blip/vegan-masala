## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups potatoes (cubed, about 3 medium)
- ¼ cup green peas (optional)
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 sprig curry leaves (optional)
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
- 2 green chilies (chopped)
- 1 teaspoon ginger paste
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons coriander leaves (finely chopped)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 6 slices bread
- ¾ cup gram flour (besan, can use coarse for added texture)
- 2 to 3 tablespoons rice flour (or cornstarch)
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ to ¾ teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain or vaamu)
- Water (as needed)
- oil (as needed for shallow or deep frying)

## Method
1. Steam or boil 1 ½ cups potatoes until just tender. If using, cook the ¼ cup green peas with them, then drain well.
2. Peel and mash the potatoes in a bowl. Leave them a little coarse so the filling has body.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a pan. Add 1 sprig curry leaves, 1 pinch asafoetida, 2 green chilies, and 1 teaspoon ginger paste. Stir for a few seconds until the ginger loses its raw smell.
4. Add ½ teaspoon red chili powder, ½ teaspoon garam masala, and ⅛ teaspoon turmeric. Stir briefly so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add the mashed potatoes, ¼ cup green peas, ½ teaspoon salt, and 3 tablespoons coriander leaves. Mix well, then finish with 1 tablespoon lemon juice. Taste and adjust the salt if needed.
6. Lay out 6 slices bread. Spread the potato filling on 3 slices, cover with the remaining slices, press lightly, and cut each sandwich into triangles if you like.
7. In a bowl, mix ¾ cup gram flour, 2 to 3 tablespoons rice flour, ½ teaspoon red chili powder, ⅛ teaspoon turmeric, ½ teaspoon salt, and ½ to ¾ teaspoon carom seeds.
8. Add water gradually and whisk to a thick, smooth batter. It should coat the bread firmly and drip off slowly.
9. Heat oil in a pan for shallow or deep frying. Test a little batter first; it should rise steadily and colour slowly.
10. Dip each bread sandwich in the batter, let the excess fall back into the bowl, and slide it into the hot oil. Fry a few at a time without crowding the pan.
11. Cook on medium heat until one side is golden and crisp, then turn and fry the other side. Drain on a rack or paper.
12. For air frying, make the batter slightly thicker, coat the sandwiches, and place them on parchment. Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8 minutes, turn carefully, then air fry for 6 to 7 minutes more until crisp and browned.

## Notes
- A slightly coarse mash gives the filling better structure and keeps the bread from turning pasty. If it seems wet, cook it for a minute longer before assembling.
- The batter should cling in a thin, even layer. Too thick and it eats like dough; too thin and it will not give a proper crust.
- Fry on medium heat so the bread cooks through as the coating turns golden. If the oil is too hot, the outside will darken before the centre heats.
- These are best eaten soon after frying. Leftovers can be reheated in an oven or air fryer, but the crust will soften a little.
- Keep the potato filling covered in the fridge for 1 to 2 days. Assemble the sandwiches only when you are ready to fry.
- Plain white bread usually needs a little more seasoning in the filling than a robust brown loaf, so taste before stuffing.
