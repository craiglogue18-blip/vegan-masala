## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 600 g mixed seasonal vegetables, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and 1 green chilli, finely chopped. Cook until the onion is soft and translucent.
3. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 2 tsp coriander powder and 1/2 tsp turmeric powder. Stir for a few seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add 600 g mixed seasonal vegetables and 1 tsp salt. Mix well and cook for a few minutes until the vegetables are coated in the masala.
6. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, with a splash of water if the pan looks dry. Cover and simmer until the vegetables are tender and the tofu has absorbed the masala.
7. Finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed, then serve warm.

## Notes
- Cook the onion until it loses its raw edge; that gives the masala body and keeps the dish from tasting thin.
- Add only a little water if needed. The vegetables should soften and stay intact, and the finished dish should remain spoonable rather than soupy.
- Taste near the end and adjust salt after the tofu has simmered, since it will mellow the seasoning slightly.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a small splash of water so the vegetables do not break down.
- Use vegetables that cook at a similar pace, or cut firmer ones a little smaller so they finish evenly in the pan.
