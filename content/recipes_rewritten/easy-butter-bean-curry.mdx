## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch ground cloves
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tbsp ground cumin
- ½ tbsp ground coriander
- 2 large tomatoes, chopped
- ½ tsp salt
- 4 cups cooked butter beans (about two 400 g tins, drained)
- 2¼ cups water
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1½–3 tbsp lemon juice

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
2. Stir in 3 cloves garlic, crushed, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 minute, stirring often so the garlic does not take on colour.
3. Add ¼ tsp cinnamon, a pinch of ground cloves, ¼ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala, ½ tbsp ground cumin, and ½ tbsp ground coriander. Stir for 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
4. Add 2 large tomatoes, chopped, and ½ tsp salt. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring and mashing the tomatoes, until the masala looks thick and the oil starts to show at the edges.
5. Stir in 4 cups cooked butter beans, 2¼ cups water, ¼ tsp cayenne, and ¼ tsp black pepper. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat.
6. Simmer for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sauce thickens and clings to the beans. If it gets too tight, add a splash of water.
7. Take the pan off the heat and stir in 1½ tbsp lemon juice. Taste and add up to 1½ tbsp more if needed, then adjust salt if necessary.

## Notes
- Cook the onion well before adding the spices; that base gives the curry its depth and keeps the sauce from tasting raw.
- Let the tomatoes reduce until the masala looks glossy and thick. If they still taste sharp, give them a few more minutes before adding the beans.
- Butter beans soften the sauce as they simmer, so check the texture near the end. Add only a little extra water if the curry becomes too dense.
- The lemon goes in at the end to keep the sauce bright. Start with less, then adjust after tasting.
- Leftovers keep well for 3 days in the fridge and usually thicken as they sit. Reheat gently with a splash of water.
- If using dried butter beans, cook them fully first so they stay intact in the curry rather than turning mushy.
