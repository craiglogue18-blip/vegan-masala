## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp rapeseed oil
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp ginger root, grated
- 1 tbsp korma curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp coriander
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 300 g swede, diced (cut smaller for even cooking)
- 300 g parsnips, diced
- 300 g carrots, diced
- 200 g potatoes, diced
- 1.2 L vegetable stock
- Freshly ground salt, to taste
- Freshly ground pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat 4 tbsp rapeseed oil in a large pan over a gentle heat. Add 2 diced onions, 4 crushed garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger root. Cover and sweat for about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onions are soft and translucent.
2. Stir in 1 tbsp korma curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander and ½ tsp garam masala. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring so the spices bloom in the oil and lose their raw edge.
3. Add 300 g swede, 300 g parsnips, 300 g carrots and 200 g potatoes. Stir well for a few minutes so the vegetables are coated in the spice mixture.
4. Pour in 1.2 L vegetable stock. Season with freshly ground salt and freshly ground pepper. Bring to the boil, then lower to a simmer, cover and cook for 25-30 minutes, until the vegetables are fully tender and the swede breaks down easily when pressed.
5. Blend the soup until smooth, or leave some texture if you want a more rustic bowl. Taste and adjust the seasoning before serving.
6. Reheat gently if needed, without letting it boil hard once blended.

## Notes
- A full blend gives a smooth, thick soup; a partial blend leaves more shape from the carrots, parsnips and potatoes.
- The soup keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the hob and add a splash of water or stock if it thickens too much.
- Freeze in portions once cool. Defrost fully before reheating so the texture stays even.
- If the finished soup tastes muted, add a little more salt rather than extra spice; the sweetness of the roots should stay clear.
