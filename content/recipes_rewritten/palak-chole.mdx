## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g spinach, chopped
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and light golden.
2. Stir in 4 garlic cloves, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 1 minute, stirring often.
3. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, and cook until it breaks down and the masala looks glossy with a little oil at the edges.
4. Add 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, and 1 tsp salt. Cook for 30 to 60 seconds.
5. Add 200 g spinach, chopped, and stir until it wilts into the masala.
6. Add 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed, and 1 cup water. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until the gravy thickens.
7. Mash a small handful of the chickpeas against the side of the pan so the curry has a little more body.
8. Turn off the heat and stir in 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder), and 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves.
9. Taste and adjust salt or amchur if needed, then serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion and tomato properly; that base gives the curry its savoury depth and keeps the spinach from tasting flat.
- If the gravy tightens too much, loosen it with a splash of hot water. If it stays thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes longer.
- A few mashed chickpeas give the sauce body, but leave some whole for a better bite.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. The spinach softens further, so reheat gently over low heat with a little water.
- Add the amchur at the end so its sharpness stays clean. If your tomatoes are very sour, use a little less and taste before adding more.
