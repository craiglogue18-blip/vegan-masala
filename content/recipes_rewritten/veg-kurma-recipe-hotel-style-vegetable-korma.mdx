## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 10 curry leaves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 cloves
- 3 green cardamoms
- 1/2 tsp peppercorns
- 1 tsp poppy seeds (optional)
- 1 cup desiccated or fresh coconut
- 1/2 cup cashews, soaked
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 cups water, divided
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 large potato, cubed
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tin beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup green peas
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Blend 1 cup desiccated or fresh coconut, 1/2 cup soaked cashews, 1 tsp fennel seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 3 cloves, 3 green cardamoms, 1/2 tsp peppercorns, 1 tsp poppy seeds if using, 4 garlic cloves, 1 inch ginger, 2 green chillies, and 1 cup water to a smooth paste.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pot over medium heat. Add 10 curry leaves and 1 large finely chopped onion, and cook until the onion softens and turns lightly golden at the edges.
3. Stir in 2 chopped carrots, 1 medium chopped tomato, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1 tsp chilli powder. Cook until the tomato breaks down and the masala looks thick and reduced.
4. Add the blended paste and cook, stirring often, until it becomes dense and the oil begins to show at the sides.
5. Lower the heat and mix in 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt, stirring until smooth.
6. Add 1 small cauliflower cut into florets, 1 large cubed potato, 1 tin chickpeas drained and rinsed, 1 tin beans drained and rinsed, and 1 cup green peas.
7. Pour in the remaining 1 cup water and add 1 tsp salt. Cover and cook until the vegetables are tender but still hold their shape.
8. Uncover and simmer until the kurma settles into a medium, creamy sauce.
9. Finish with 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander and serve hot.
10. Taste and adjust salt only after the vegetables are cooked through.

## Notes
- Take the onion past the raw stage before the vegetables go in; that base gives the curry its depth.
- Let the ground masala cook down properly before adding the coconut vegetables. If it stays loose, the curry will taste thin.
- Keep the heat low after adding plant yoghurt so it blends in smoothly.
- The sauce thickens as it sits. For leftovers, loosen with a splash of water and reheat gently.
- This keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days and holds its body better the next day.
