## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp ajwain (optional)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté mode and heat 2 tbsp oil.
2. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp ajwain, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 2 green cardamom pods and 4 cloves. Stir for a few seconds until the spices release their aroma.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges.
4. Stir in 1 inch ginger, grated, 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 30 to 40 seconds.
5. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down and the masala turns thick and pulpy.
6. Stir in 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chilli powder and cook for 1 minute, stirring so the spices lose their raw taste.
7. Add 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed, 1 tsp salt and 1 cup water. Scrape the bottom of the pot well.
8. Close the lid and pressure cook on high for 5 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then release any remaining pressure.
9. Open the lid and switch back to sauté mode. Simmer until the gravy thickens to your liking.
10. Mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pot for a thicker gravy, or leave them whole if you want a looser sauce.
11. Stir in 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
12. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion and tomato base until the raw edge is gone; that is what gives the gravy depth.
- The pressure-cooked gravy will thicken a little as it stands. Add a splash of hot water if it gets too tight.
- Mashing a small portion of the chickpeas gives the sauce more body without needing extra ingredients.
- Taste after pressure cooking before adding the lemon juice; if it needs more brightness, add a little more at the table.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and often taste better the next day.
- Reheat gently on the stove or in the microwave, loosening with a little water if needed.
