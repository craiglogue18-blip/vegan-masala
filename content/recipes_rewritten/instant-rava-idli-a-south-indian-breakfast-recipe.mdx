## Ingredients
- 1 cup semolina / rava
- 1/2 cup rice flour
- 2 cups unsweetened plant-based yogurt
- 1/2 to 1 1/2 cups plant-based milk
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- 1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, minced
- salt, to taste
- 1/2 tsp coconut oil
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 2-3 green chillies, minced
- 6-7 curry leaves
- 1/4 tsp asafetida

## Method
1. Dry roast 1 cup semolina / rava and 1/2 cup rice flour in a wok over medium-low heat until the mixture turns pale golden and smells nutty. Take it off the heat and let it cool for a few minutes.
2. Add 2 cups unsweetened plant-based yogurt, 1/4 tsp baking soda, 2 tsp minced ginger, 1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, and salt to taste. Mix well, then pour in 1/2 to 1 1/2 cups plant-based milk a little at a time until you have a batter that is just looser than pancake batter.
3. Heat 1/2 tsp coconut oil in a small pan. Add 1/2 tsp mustard seeds and let them splutter, then add 2-3 green chillies, 6-7 curry leaves, and 1/4 tsp asafetida. Pour this tempering into the batter and fold through gently.
4. Rest the batter for at least 15 minutes so the semolina softens properly. Grease the idli plates, fill each mould almost to the top, and steam for 10 to 12 minutes until the idlis are set and spring back lightly when touched.
5. Let the idlis sit for a minute before unmoulding. Serve warm with chutney or sambar.

## Notes
- The resting time matters here; it gives the rava time to soften and keeps the crumb light rather than sandy. If the batter thickens while standing, loosen it with a splash of plant-based milk before steaming.
- Steam on steady heat and do not overcook, or the idlis can turn dry at the edges. A toothpick should come out clean, but the surface should still feel soft.
- Leftover idlis keep well in the fridge for 1 to 2 days. Reheat in a steamer or covered in the microwave with a few drops of water so they stay moist.
- For a sharper bite, add more green chillies. Finely grated carrot or courgette can be mixed in with the batter if you want a little more body without changing the method much.
