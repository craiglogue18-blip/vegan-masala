## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp olive or groundnut oil
- ½ tsp brown mustard seeds
- 1 hot green chilli, finely chopped
- 100g/4oz potato, peeled and cut into 5mm/¼in dice
- ¼ carrot, peeled and cut into 5mm/¼in dice
- 40g/1½oz green beans, cut into 5mm/¼in segments
- thumb-sized piece of fresh ginger, peeled and finely grated
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp cardamom seeds, removed from cardamom pods
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp black cumin seeds (or regular cumin seeds)
- 1 tsp whole cloves
- 1/3 of a whole nutmeg, broken
- a medium stick of cinnamon, about 5cm-8cm/2in-3in, broken into 3-4 pieces
- basmati rice, measured to the 450ml/1 pint level
- 1¼ tsp salt
- 570ml/1 pint water

## Method
1. Rinse the basmati rice measured to the 450ml/1 pint level several times, until the water runs clear. Soak it in fresh water for 30 minutes, then drain well.
2. Heat 3 tbsp olive or groundnut oil in a heavy pan with a tight-fitting lid over medium-high heat. Add ½ tsp brown mustard seeds and let them begin to pop.
3. Add 1 finely chopped hot green chilli, 100g/4oz diced potato, ¼ diced carrot and 40g/1½oz green beans. Stir for 4 to 5 minutes, until the vegetables are glossy and the edges start to take colour.
4. Add the grated thumb-sized piece of ginger, ½ tsp ground turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tbsp cardamom seeds, 1 tsp black peppercorns, 1 tsp black cumin seeds, 1 tsp whole cloves, 1/3 of a whole nutmeg and the broken cinnamon stick. Stir for 1 minute so the spices bloom without catching.
5. Tip in the drained rice and stir gently for 2 minutes, coating the grains in the spiced oil.
6. Add 1¼ tsp salt and 570ml/1 pint water. Bring to a boil, cover tightly, lower the heat to very low and cook for 25 minutes without lifting the lid.
7. Take the pan off the heat and leave it covered for 10 minutes. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot.

## Notes
- A tight lid matters here. If your pan lid is loose, press a sheet of foil over the pan before fitting the lid so the steam stays in and the rice cooks evenly.
- The rice should finish separate and tender, with no wet patch at the base. If it still feels firm after resting, add a spoonful of hot water, cover again and leave it for 5 minutes.
- For leftovers, cool the rice quickly and keep it covered in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat with a small splash of water in a covered pan or in the microwave so the grains loosen again.
- Keep any vegetable swaps small and even so they cook at the same pace as the rice. Peas, broccoli florets or diced peppers work well in place of the carrot or beans.
