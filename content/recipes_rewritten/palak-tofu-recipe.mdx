## Ingredients
- 200 g spinach
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 30 g cashews
- 1 cup water, divided
- 2 tbsp oil
- whole spices (optional): cumin seeds, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, cloves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
- 100 ml oat cream

## Method
1. Put 200 g spinach, 2 green chillies, 30 g cashews and 2 tbsp of the 1 cup water in a pan and cook just until the spinach wilts.
2. Blend the spinach mixture to a smooth puree and set it aside.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in the same pan over medium heat. Add the whole spices, if using, and let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
4. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook slowly until soft and lightly golden.
5. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and cook for about 30 seconds, just until the raw smell fades.
6. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, and cook until it breaks down and turns jammy.
7. Mix in 1 tsp garam masala and stir for a few seconds so it blooms in the masala.
8. Pour in the remaining water from the 1 cup and add 1 tsp salt, then simmer until the onion is fully soft and the masala has some body.
9. Stir in the spinach puree and simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring now and then.
10. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, and cook for 3 to 5 minutes so it takes on the gravy.
11. Finish with 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves and 100 ml oat cream. Stir through, then turn off the heat and let it sit for a minute before serving.

## Notes
- Take the onion-tomato base all the way to soft and jammy before the spinach goes in. That step gives the curry its depth and keeps the greens tasting clean.
- The cashews help the gravy hold together without feeling heavy. If you want a thinner sauce, add a little more water at the end rather than cutting the cashews out.
- Use firm tofu so it keeps its shape in the simmer. If your tofu is very soft, add it at the end and warm it through more gently.
- Taste after the tofu has heated through and adjust salt only then. The spinach, cream and tofu all change how the seasoning reads.
- Leftovers keep well for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat slowly with a splash of water, since the gravy tightens as it cools.
