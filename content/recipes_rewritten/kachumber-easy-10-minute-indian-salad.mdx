## Ingredients
- 1 cup cucumber, chopped
- 1 cup tomato, chopped
- 1/2 cup red onion, chopped
- 1 green chili pepper, small, optional, chopped
- 1 tablespoon cilantro leaves, chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
- 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder (mirchi powder)
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Method
1. Wash and chop 1 cup cucumber, 1 cup tomato, 1/2 cup red onion, and the small optional 1 green chili pepper into small pieces.
2. Put the cucumber, tomato, onion, and chili in a bowl with 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro leaves.
3. Add 1/2 teaspoon roasted cumin powder, 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder, 2 teaspoons lemon juice, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
4. Toss gently so the vegetables stay crisp, then taste and adjust with a little more salt or lemon if needed.
5. Serve at once, with a few extra cilantro leaves on top if you like.

## Notes
- Make kachumber just before serving. Salt draws liquid from the cucumber and tomato, so it softens quickly if it sits around.
- If the tomatoes are very watery, seed them first. That keeps the salad bright and stops it from turning soupy.
- The salad can be kept in the fridge for up to 1 day, covered. Stir well before serving and pour off any excess liquid.
- For a sharper salad, add a little more lemon at the end. For more heat, keep the green chili in; for a milder bowl, leave it out.
