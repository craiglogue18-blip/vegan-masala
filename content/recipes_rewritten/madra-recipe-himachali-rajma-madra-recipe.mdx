## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 4-5 whole cloves
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 green cardamoms
- a pinch of asafoetida (optional)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Drain and rinse 1 tin kidney beans and set them aside.
2. Whisk 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt until smooth and keep it ready.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 small cinnamon stick, 4-5 whole cloves, 1 bay leaf, 4 green cardamoms and a pinch of asafoetida, if using. Let the spices sizzle for about 30 seconds.
4. Add 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp chilli powder and 2 tsp coriander powder. Stir for a few seconds until the spices darken slightly and smell cooked.
5. Lower the heat and slowly whisk in 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt, stirring constantly so it stays smooth.
6. Cook the masala gently for 2-3 minutes, until it thickens and the oil begins to show at the edges.
7. Add the kidney beans, 1 cup water and 1 tsp salt. Stir well, then simmer on low for 10-12 minutes, until the curry turns creamy and the masala settles around the beans.
8. Taste and adjust the salt if needed, then serve hot.

## Notes
- Keep the heat low once the yoghurt goes in. If the pan is too hot, the masala can turn grainy or split.
- The curry should be creamy but not soupy. If it thickens too much on standing, loosen it with a splash of hot water.
- Check seasoning after the simmer, since the beans and reduction change how much salt the dish needs.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat best over low heat with a little water.
- The flavour settles after a short rest, so the curry is useful for making ahead earlier in the day.
