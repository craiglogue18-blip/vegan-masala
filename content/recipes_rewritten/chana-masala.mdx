## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder (or to taste)
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp salt, or to taste
- 120 ml water, plus more if needed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped and divided

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add 1 finely chopped onion and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring often, until soft, golden and lightly sweet.
2. Add 3 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 tbsp grated ginger. Cook for 30 to 45 seconds, stirring, until the raw smell fades.
3. Stir in 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder and 2 tsp garam masala. Fry for 15 to 20 seconds, stirring so the spices do not catch.
4. Add 1 can chopped tomatoes and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes break down and the masala looks darker and thicker.
5. Add 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed, 1 tsp salt and 120 ml water. Stir well, then mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pan to help the sauce thicken.
6. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are well coated and the sauce clings to them. Add a splash more water if it starts to stick before the chickpeas are ready.
7. Stir in 1 tbsp lemon juice and most of the 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Taste and adjust salt or chilli if needed.
8. Scatter over the remaining coriander and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion well before adding anything else; that slow base gives the gravy its body and keeps the tomatoes from tasting sharp.
- For a thicker chana masala, mash more chickpeas into the pan near the end. For a looser curry, add a little extra water while it simmers.
- The sauce thickens as it rests, so let it sit for 5 minutes before serving if it looks a little loose.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days in a sealed container. Reheat gently with a splash of water and stir from the bottom of the pan.
- This freezes well for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and warm through on the stove rather than boiling hard.
