## Ingredients
- 2 cups atta flour (or whole wheat flour), plus extra for dusting
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3/4-1 cup water

## Method
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine 2 cups atta flour and 1 teaspoon salt.
2. Slowly add 3/4 cup water, mixing with a spatula or your hand until the flour starts to gather into a dough. Add a little more water only if the dough still looks dry and crumbly.
3. Knead the dough for 3 to 5 minutes until smooth and elastic. If it feels sticky, dust in a little more atta flour as you knead.
4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and rest for 25 to 30 minutes.
5. Knead the rested dough briefly, then divide it into 8 equal portions.
6. Shape one portion into a smooth ball and flatten it lightly with your palm.
7. Dust the work surface and rolling pin with extra atta flour, then roll the dough into an oval. Turn it 90 degrees and roll again into a circle about 5 to 6 inches across.
8. Repeat with the remaining portions, dusting lightly as needed so the dough does not stick.
9. Heat a cast-iron or non-stick pan over medium-high heat until hot.
10. Place one chapati on the pan and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until small bubbles appear and the surface starts to lift. Flip and cook the second side for about 1 minute, until light golden spots appear.
11. Cook the remaining chapatis the same way. For more puff, place each chapati briefly over an open flame after both sides are cooked, if you have a gas hob.

## Notes
- Atta gives a softer, more flexible chapati than standard whole wheat flour, so use it if you can.
- The dough should feel soft and pliable, not tight. A stiff dough rolls poorly and cracks at the edges.
- If the dough feels dry after resting, wet your hands lightly and knead for a few seconds rather than adding too much extra flour.
- A properly heated pan is important. If it is too cool, the chapati dries out before the surface cooks.
- Keep the cooked chapatis wrapped in a clean kitchen towel as you finish the batch so they stay soft.
- Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat on a hot pan for a few seconds on each side until supple again.
