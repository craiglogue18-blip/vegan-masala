## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 2 carrots, peeled and cut into cubes
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 20g/¾oz fresh root ginger, peeled and grated
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 heaped tbsp garam masala
- 200g/7oz dried split red lentils, rinsed and drained
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 600ml/21fl oz reduced salt vegetable stock (gluten-free if necessary)
- 500g/1lb 2oz sweet potatoes, cut into cubes
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into small florets
- 150g/5½oz frozen green beans or peas
- salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 300g/10½oz cooked basmati rice
- plain unsweetened vegan yoghurt (optional)
- flatbreads (optional)
- handful fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200C/180C Fan/Gas 6.
2. Put 500g sweet potatoes and 1 small cauliflower on a baking tray with ½ tbsp of the oil and a little salt and pepper. Toss well, then roast for 20–25 minutes until the edges are browned.
3. While the vegetables roast, heat the remaining ½ tbsp oil in a large casserole pan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and lightly golden.
4. Add 2 carrots, 3 garlic cloves, 20g ginger, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 heaped tbsp garam masala. Cook for 1 minute until the spices smell fragrant.
5. Stir in 200g red lentils, 400g chopped tomatoes and 600ml vegetable stock. Bring to a gentle boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally so the lentils do not catch.
6. Add the roasted sweet potatoes and cauliflower to the pan. Simmer for 10 minutes, or until the lentils are soft and the sauce has a thick, creamy body.
7. Stir in 150g frozen green beans or peas and cook for 5 minutes more. Add a splash of water if the dhansak becomes too thick.
8. Taste and season with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Serve with 300g cooked basmati rice, a spoonful of vegan yoghurt if using, flatbreads and chopped coriander.

## Notes
- Dhansak should be thick and spoonable, with the lentils soft enough to break down but still holding some shape. If it tightens on standing, loosen it with a little hot water when reheating.
- This keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days and freezes for about 1 month. Reheat gently so the lentils do not dry out.
- The roasted vegetables can be swapped with other sturdy vegetables in season, but keep the sauce thick enough to hold them.
