## Ingredients
- 14 ounces firm or extra-firm tofu
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon salt, divided
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons mustard oil or neutral oil, for cooking the tofu
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1½ cups chopped red onion
- ½ teaspoon fennel seeds
- ½ teaspoon black peppercorns
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 to 2 tablespoons Kashmiri chili powder
- 1 black cardamom, slightly opened
- 2 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick, 1½ to 2 inches long
- generous pinch asafoetida (hing), optional
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- 4 ounces tomato puree or passata
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup water
- cilantro, for garnish
- lemon juice, for garnish, optional to taste

## Method
1. Press the 14 ounces firm or extra-firm tofu, then cut it into 1½- to 2-inch squares about ½ inch thick, or tear it into irregular pieces if you want more edges for the sauce to catch.
2. In a shallow bowl, mix 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ¼ teaspoon of the salt, and 1 tablespoon cornstarch. Add the tofu and turn gently until lightly coated on all sides.
3. Set a large skillet over medium-high heat and add 2 teaspoons mustard oil or neutral oil. When the oil is hot, lay in the tofu in a single layer and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until golden. Remove to a plate.
4. Return the skillet to medium-high heat and add 2 teaspoons oil. Add 1½ cups chopped red onion, ½ teaspoon fennel seeds, ½ teaspoon black peppercorns, and the remaining ¼ teaspoon salt. Cook, stirring often, until the onions turn deep golden, adding small splashes of water if they start to catch.
5. Stir in 3 cloves minced garlic and cook for 30 to 45 seconds, just until the raw smell softens. Transfer the mixture to a blender, add 1.5 to 2 tablespoons Kashmiri chili powder and 1 cup water, and blend smooth.
6. Wipe out the skillet if needed and set it over medium heat. Add 1 black cardamom, 2 whole cloves, and 1 cinnamon stick to the pan with a little warmth for a few seconds, then add a generous pinch of asafoetida if using, followed by the blended onion paste, 4 ounces tomato puree, ¼ teaspoon turmeric, ½ teaspoon ground ginger, and ½ teaspoon ground cumin.
7. Stir well and cook until the masala darkens slightly and the tomato loses its raw edge. Add the fried tofu and ½ cup water, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 20 to 30 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the sauce thickens and coats the tofu.
8. Taste and adjust with a little more salt if needed. Finish with cilantro and a few drops of lemon juice, then serve warm.
9. If the sauce gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of hot water and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes more.

## Notes
- Tearing the tofu gives more texture and helps the sauce cling; neat cubes work well too.
- If you have time, coat the tofu and let it sit for 20 to 30 minutes before frying. The spices settle into the surface and the crust holds better.
- The onion base should cook to a deep golden brown, not pale. That step builds the body of the curry.
- The sauce thickens as it sits. For a looser curry, add a little hot water before serving.
- Leftovers keep for up to 3 days in the refrigerator and reheat gently on the stove with a splash of water.
- Serve with basmati rice, naan, or roti; it also works well tucked into wraps with sliced onion and herbs.
