## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups besan (fine chickpea flour)
- 1 teaspoon sea salt flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/4 teaspoon ground red chilli (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon ajwain (carom seeds)
- 1 1/2 cups warm water
- olive oil, for pan frying
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium tomato, deseeded and finely chopped
- 1 long green chilli, deseeded and thinly sliced
- a handful of fresh coriander leaves, chopped

## Method
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together 1 1/2 cups besan, 1 teaspoon sea salt flakes, 1/2 teaspoon baking powder, 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric, 1 teaspoon ground coriander, 1/4 teaspoon ground red chilli, if using, and 1/2 teaspoon ajwain.
2. Pour in 1 1/2 cups warm water gradually, whisking until the batter is smooth and pourable. Rest it for 5 to 10 minutes so the besan hydrates and the batter loosens slightly.
3. Heat a medium crepe pan over medium-low heat and add about 1/2 teaspoon olive oil. When the pan is hot, pour in about 1/2 cup batter and swirl it into an 18 to 20 cm circle.
4. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until the surface looks mostly set and small bubbles appear. Scatter some of the 1 red onion, 1 medium tomato, 1 long green chilli and chopped coriander over the top.
5. Flip carefully and cook the vegetable side for 1 to 2 minutes, until the second side is golden and the vegetables are warmed through.
6. Slide onto a plate and serve warm. Repeat with the remaining batter and vegetables, adding a little more olive oil to the pan as needed.

## Notes
- Resting the batter helps the besan soften and gives a cleaner, less gritty texture. If it thickens too much while standing, loosen it with a spoonful of warm water.
- Keep the pan at medium-low to start; if the heat is too high, the outside sets before the centre cooks properly.
- Leftover omelettes keep in the fridge for up to 2 days. Reheat in a dry pan rather than the microwave so the edges stay a little crisp.
- For a milder version, leave out the red chilli and use less green chilli. For a firmer pancake, let the batter sit a few minutes longer before cooking.
