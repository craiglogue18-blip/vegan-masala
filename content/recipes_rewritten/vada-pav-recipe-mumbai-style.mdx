## Ingredients
- ½ cup tamarind (tightly packed & seedless)
- 1.75 cups hot water
- 2 teaspoons oil, divided
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- ½ teaspoon ginger powder (ground ginger)
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
- ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder
- 7 to 8 tablespoons jaggery powder (adjust to taste, can swap with sugar)
- salt or black salt, as required
- 350 grams potatoes (2 medium to large)
- 1 to 2 green chillies
- 6 to 7 cloves garlic (small to medium-sized)
- ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder
- 7 to 8 curry leaves
- 1 to 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves (cilantro)
- 1 to 1.25 cups gram flour (besan)
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
- 1 pinch baking soda (optional)
- ½ cup water (or add as required)
- oil, as required for deep frying
- 1 cup chopped coriander leaves
- 1 to 2 cloves garlic (chopped)
- 2 to 3 drops lemon juice
- 2 to 3 green chillies (chopped)
- salt, as required
- 2 to 3 drops lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons dry garlic chutney (optional)
- 8 pav (dinner rolls)
- 3 to 4 fried green chillies (mixed with some salt, optional)
- salt, for the fried chillies, optional

## Method
1. Soak ½ cup tamarind in 1.75 cups hot water for 30 to 40 minutes. Squeeze out the pulp well and strain it into a bowl.
2. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a small pan on low heat. Add ½ teaspoon cumin seeds and let them crackle, then stir in ½ teaspoon ginger powder, ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder, and 1 pinch asafoetida for a few seconds.
3. Add the tamarind pulp and cook for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in 7 to 8 tablespoons jaggery powder and salt or black salt, then simmer until the chutney thickens. Set aside to cool.
4. Boil or steam 350 grams potatoes until fork-tender. Peel and mash them while still warm.
5. Crush 1 to 2 green chillies and 6 to 7 cloves garlic to a semi-fine paste with a mortar and pestle.
6. Heat the remaining 1 teaspoon oil in a small pan. Add ½ teaspoon mustard seeds and let them pop, then add 7 to 8 curry leaves, 1 pinch asafoetida, and ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder.
7. Add the green chilli-garlic paste and cook just until the raw smell fades. Keep the garlic pale.
8. Mix the tempering into the mashed potatoes with 1 to 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves and salt. Shape into small to medium flattened patties and keep them aside.
9. In a bowl, whisk 1 to 1.25 cups gram flour, ⅛ teaspoon turmeric powder, 1 pinch asafoetida, and 1 pinch baking soda if using. Add ½ cup water gradually to make a thick, smooth batter that coats the potato patties.
10. Heat oil for deep frying in a kadai. Dip each potato patty into the batter and coat it evenly.
11. Fry the coated vadas in hot oil until golden and crisp, turning as needed for even colour. Drain on paper towels.
12. If using, fry 3 to 4 green chillies in the same oil until blistered, then sprinkle with salt.
13. Blend 1 cup chopped coriander leaves, 1 to 2 cloves chopped garlic, 2 to 3 drops lemon juice, 2 to 3 chopped green chillies, and salt to a coarse chutney.
14. Split 8 pav without cutting all the way through. Spread green chutney, tamarind chutney, and 2 tablespoons dry garlic chutney, if using, inside each roll.
15. Place one hot vada in each pav and serve at once with the fried green chillies.
16. Serve right away so the pav stays soft and the vada keeps its crisp coating.

## Notes
- Make the tamarind chutney ahead if you can; it thickens as it cools and tastes cleaner once the jaggery settles in.
- The potato mixture should hold its shape without cracking. If it feels soft, mash it a little more and let it cool before shaping.
- Besan batter should coat the potato lightly but fully. If it is too thick, add water a spoon at a time; if it is too thin, the coating will slide off.
- Keep the oil steady and hot enough for frying. If the vadas colour too fast, the outside will darken before the batter cooks through.
- For serving later, keep the chutneys, vadas, and pav separate. Reheat the vadas in a hot oven or air fryer, then assemble at the table.
- Leftover green chutney keeps for a day or two in the fridge. The pav is best fresh, but the fillings can be made earlier in the day.
