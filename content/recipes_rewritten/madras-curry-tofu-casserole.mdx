## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1/2 tsp peppercorns
- 2 dried red chillies
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 curry leaves
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground mustard
- 200 ml passata or smooth tomato purée
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. In a mortar or spice grinder, crush 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds, 1/2 tsp peppercorns, 2 dried red chillies and 2 green cardamom pods to a coarse powder.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in an ovenproof casserole over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 6 curry leaves, then cook for 1 minute. Add the crushed spices, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin and 1 tsp ground mustard, and stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add 200 ml passata or smooth tomato purée and 1 medium tomato, chopped. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring often, until the tomato loses its raw edge and the masala thickens.
6. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, 200 g mushrooms, sliced, and 1 tsp salt. Stir well so the tofu and mushrooms are coated in the masala.
7. Pour in 1 tin coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring to combine.
8. Transfer to the oven and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until the mushrooms are tender and the sauce has reduced slightly.
9. Stir in 1 tbsp lemon juice and finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Taste and adjust salt if needed before serving.

## Notes
- Take time with the onion and tomato base. Once the onion has softened and the tomato has cooked down, the sauce will taste rounded rather than sharp.
- If the masala seems too loose before it goes into the oven, simmer it on the hob for a few minutes first. If it tightens too much while baking, loosen it with a splash of hot water.
- Firm tofu keeps its shape in this dish. If you want a drier bite, press it for 15 to 20 minutes before cubing.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the hob or in a covered dish in the oven so the coconut milk stays smooth.
- The flavour deepens overnight, so this is a good casserole to make ahead and reheat for the next meal.
