## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- zest of 1 lime
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
2. In a large bowl, combine 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp curry powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper and 1 inch grated ginger.
3. Add 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets, and toss well so the spice mixture coats the florets evenly.
4. Spread the cauliflower in a single layer on a baking tray.
5. Roast for 10 to 15 minutes, turning once if needed, until the edges are browned and the centres are tender.
6. Finish with the zest of 1 lime and 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander, then serve straight away.

## Notes
- Spread the florets out well on the tray; crowding will soften them before they brown. Use two trays if needed.
- Add the lime zest and coriander after roasting so the finish stays fresh and bright.
- Taste after roasting and adjust with a little more salt or lime if needed. The seasoning should stay clean, not heavy.
- Leftovers keep for up to 3 days in the fridge. Reheat in a hot oven or a dry pan so the edges stay a little crisp.
