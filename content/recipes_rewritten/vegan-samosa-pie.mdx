## Ingredients
- 1 sheet puff pastry
- 1 tsp oil
- ¼ tsp cumin seeds
- ¼ tsp coriander seeds
- ¼ cup red onion, chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp cashews, chopped
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¾ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp cayenne (to taste)
- 2 cups potatoes, cubed
- ½ tsp salt
- ⅓ cup water
- ¼ cup green peas
- 2 tbsp chopped coriander
- 2 tbsp chopped mint
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp chaat masala or amchur
- Optional greens or chutney for serving

## Method
1. Take the 1 sheet puff pastry out of the fridge while you make the filling, so it stays cold but pliable.
2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add ¼ tsp cumin seeds and ¼ tsp coriander seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add ¼ cup red onion, chopped, 1 green chilli, finely chopped, and 2 tbsp cashews, chopped. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring often, until the onion softens.
4. Stir in 2 tsp minced ginger, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¾ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp ground coriander, and ¼ tsp cayenne. Cook for about 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
5. Add 2 cups potatoes, cubed, ½ tsp salt, and ⅓ cup water. Cover and cook until the potatoes are tender and the water has mostly evaporated.
6. Mash some of the potatoes with the back of the spoon, then stir in ¼ cup green peas, 2 tbsp chopped coriander, 2 tbsp chopped mint, 1 tsp lemon juice, and ½ tsp chaat masala or amchur.
7. Cook uncovered for a minute or two if needed, until the filling is thick and holds together. Let it cool until just warm before filling the pastry.
8. Heat the oven to 200°C. Roll the pastry out to roughly double its size on a lightly floured surface.
9. Spoon the filling onto one half of the pastry, leaving a border around the edges. Fold the other half over, press to seal, and cut a few steam vents on top.
10. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until the pastry is deep golden and crisp underneath. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

## Notes
- The filling should be fairly dry before it goes into the pastry. If it is still loose, keep cooking it uncovered for a little longer.
- Do not mash all the potatoes. A few chunks give the filling the right body and keep the texture close to a samosa filling.
- Taste the masala while it is still warm and adjust salt, chilli, or chaat masala before it cools.
- This pie is best on the day it is baked, when the pastry is crisp and the filling is still soft inside.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot oven so the pastry regains its texture.
- Serve with chutney or simple greens to balance the spiced filling.
