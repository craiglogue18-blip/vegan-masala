## Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1.5 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 cup unsweetened soy yoghurt
- 0.25 cup unsweetened soy milk
- 1.5 Tbsp unsalted vegan butter
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. In a large bowl, mix 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1.5 Tbsp granulated sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, and 0.5 tsp salt.
2. Add 0.5 cup unsweetened soy yoghurt and 0.25 cup unsweetened soy milk, then stir until a soft dough forms.
3. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 3 to 4 minutes, until smooth and supple.
4. Divide the dough into 4 equal balls and cover them while you work.
5. Roll each ball into an oval about 0.5 cm thick.
6. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and cook each naan for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until spotted and lightly golden.
7. Melt 1.5 Tbsp unsalted vegan butter in a small pan or bowl, then stir in 2 garlic cloves, minced.
8. Brush the hot naan with the garlic butter and scatter over 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped.

## Notes
- Keep the dough soft; adding too much flour will make the naan dry and less tender.
- If the dough feels stiff while mixing, add a little more soy milk, 1 teaspoon at a time, until it relaxes.
- Cook over steady medium heat so the bread cooks through without burning before the centre sets.
- Brush with the garlic butter as soon as the naan leaves the pan so it sinks into the surface.
- These reheat well in a hot dry pan for a minute or two; they can also be wrapped and refrigerated for up to 3 days.
