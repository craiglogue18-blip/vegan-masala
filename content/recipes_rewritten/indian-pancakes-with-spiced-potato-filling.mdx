## Ingredients
- 4 potatoes (medium-large, about 500g in weight)
- 130 g gram (chickpea) flour
- 130 g plain flour
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 400 ml water
- 3 tbsp rapeseed or sunflower oil
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 1 cm root ginger, grated
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp dairy-free milk
- 1 lemon, juiced','4 spring onions, finely chopped','rapeseed or sunflower oil for frying

## Method
1. Bring a large saucepan of water to the boil. Peel the 4 potatoes, cut them into chunks and cook for 10–15 minutes, until tender. Drain well and set aside.
2. In a bowl, mix 130 g gram flour, 130 g plain flour, ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda and ½ tsp salt.
3. Lightly crush 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 tsp coriander seeds, then stir them into the flour mixture.
4. Whisk in 400 ml water a little at a time until you have a smooth batter. Blend briefly if needed to remove any lumps.
5. Heat a small frying pan over medium heat and add 3 tbsp oil. Add 1 clove garlic, 1 red chilli, 1 cm ginger, 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp turmeric, and fry gently for 2 minutes, stirring so the spices do not catch.
6. Mash the drained potatoes with 2 tbsp dairy-free milk until smooth. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Stir the spiced oil into the potatoes, then mix in the juice of 1 lemon and 4 spring onions.
8. Heat a large frying pan over high heat and brush or wipe with a little oil. Pour in a ladle of batter and swirl to coat the base.
9. Cook until the underside is golden and the top begins to set, then turn and cook the second side for about 1 minute.
10. Turn the pancake back to the first side and spread about one-sixth of the potato filling over it. Heat for 1 minute, then fold or roll and transfer to a plate.
11. Repeat with the remaining batter and filling, keeping the finished pancakes warm in a low oven until ready to serve.

## Notes
- Keep the batter fairly thin so it spreads easily in the pan. If it thickens while standing, whisk in a splash of water.
- The potato filling should be soft but not loose. Drain the potatoes well before mashing so the filling does not turn wet.
- Reheat leftovers in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat so the outside stays crisp. The filling can also be warmed separately and used as a quick sandwich filling or with flatbreads.
- Adjust the chilli and ginger to suit the table. A little extra lemon at the end lifts the filling if the potatoes taste flat.
