## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups raw basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes, preferably)
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 1/4 cups water
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp avocado oil or any neutral oil, divided
- 1 tbsp chana dal (Bengal gram dal)
- 2 tsp urad dal (Black gram dal), divided
- 2 dry red chili peppers (or 1 tsp red pepper flakes; adjust to preference)
- 1 tbsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 cup raw cashews (chopped coarsely)
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 3/4 cup shredded coconut (unsweetened)

## Method
1. Drain the soaked 1.5 cups basmati rice and put it in a saucepan with 1 cup coconut milk, 1 1/4 cups water, and salt to taste.
2. Bring to a boil, cover with a tight-fitting lid, lower the heat, and cook for about 15 minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
3. While the rice cooks, heat 1/2 tbsp oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add 1 tbsp chana dal, 1 tsp urad dal, and 2 dry red chili peppers, and toast until the dals turn golden.
4. Remove from the heat and crush or grind the toasted chana dal, urad dal, and chilies to a coarse powder. Set aside.
5. In the same skillet, heat the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil over medium heat. Add 1 tbsp mustard seeds and let them splutter.
6. Add the remaining 1 tsp urad dal, 1/4 cup chopped cashews, and 1 sprig curry leaves. Cook until the urad dal and cashews are golden and the curry leaves are crisp.
7. Stir in 3/4 cup shredded coconut and sauté briefly, just until it turns lightly golden and smells nutty.
8. Fold the coconut mixture and the ground dal powder through the cooked rice with a fork, keeping the grains separate. Taste and add a little more salt only if needed, then serve warm.

## Notes
- Soaking the rice helps the grains cook evenly and stay separate. If you skip the soak, add a splash more water and watch the pot near the end so it does not turn soft.
- The coconut needs only a light toast. Once it starts taking on colour, move it off the heat so it stays sweet and does not turn bitter.
- For the cleanest texture, fold the tempering through the rice after it has finished cooking and rested for a minute or two.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a spoon of water or a little coconut milk to loosen the grains.
- If using red pepper flakes instead of dry chilies, add them with the mustard seeds so they do not scorch.
- This rice sits well beside a dry vegetable curry or a saucy lentil dish. A few fresh curry leaves or chopped coriander on top is enough.
