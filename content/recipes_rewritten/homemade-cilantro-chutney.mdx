## Ingredients
- 1/4 of a small yellow onion, coarsely chopped
- 2-3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1-inch piece of fresh ginger, chopped
- 2-8 green chilies, chopped (2 for mild, 4-8 for spicy)
- 1 bunch cilantro, washed and cut into 1-2 inch sections, including the stems
- 1/2 cup fresh mint, coarsely chopped
- 1/2 cup vegan plain unsweetened yogurt
- 1/2-1 tsp salt, to taste
- 2-4 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon or lime juice, to taste

## Method
1. In a food processor or blender, add 1/4 of a small yellow onion, 2-3 chopped cloves garlic, 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, and 2-8 chopped green chilies. Blend until the mix is finely minced.
2. Add 1 bunch cilantro, 1/2 cup fresh mint, 1/2 cup vegan plain unsweetened yogurt, 1/2-1 tsp salt, and 2-4 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon or lime juice. Blend until smooth or leave a little texture if you prefer.
3. Taste and adjust with more salt or lemon or lime juice if needed. If the chutney is too thick, blend in a spoonful of water at a time until it loosens slightly.

## Notes
- Make this close to serving for the brightest colour and cleanest herb flavour. It keeps in a covered container in the fridge for 3 to 4 days, though the herbs will soften a little.
- The yogurt can separate slightly after chilling. Stir well before serving, and thin again with a little water if needed.
- For a sharper chutney, use the full amount of lemon or lime juice. For more heat, add more green chilies; for a thicker spread, keep the chutney on the dense side.
