## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp mustard seeds and 1 tsp cumin seeds, and let them crackle.
2. Add 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Stir for 20 to 30 seconds, just until the raw edge softens.
3. Stir in 1 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder and 2 tsp coriander powder. Cook for a few seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
4. Add 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets, and 3 medium potatoes, cubed. Turn them through the masala so the surfaces are well coated.
5. Pour in 1 cup water and add 1 tsp salt. Cover and cook on medium heat until the potatoes are tender and the cauliflower is soft but still holding together.
6. Uncover and simmer for a few minutes if needed to reduce the liquid slightly. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Keep the florets and potato cubes close in size so they finish at the same time. The dish should be lightly sauced, with the vegetables tender and intact.
- If the pan dries before the potatoes are cooked, add a splash of hot water rather than lowering the heat too much. That keeps the masala moving without sticking.
- The mustard seeds should crackle before the ginger goes in; that first sizzle gives the dish its base aroma.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a spoonful of water to loosen the masala.
- The flavour settles after resting, so this works well for cooking a little ahead.
