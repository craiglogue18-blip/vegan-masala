## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 4 whole cloves
- 4 green cardamoms
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 200 ml tomato puree
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 4 whole cloves and 4 green cardamoms. Let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until it turns deep golden and the masala looks thick and glossy.
3. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for about 30 seconds, just until the raw smell softens.
4. Add 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder and 2 tsp coriander powder. Stir for a minute so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add 200 ml tomato puree and 1 tsp salt. Cook until the tomatoes lose their raw edge and the masala starts to release oil around the sides.
6. Add 200 g mushrooms, sliced, and stir well so they are coated in the masala.
7. Pour in 1 cup water, cover, and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, until the mushrooms are tender and the gravy has some body.
8. Uncover and cook for a few minutes more if you want a thicker curry. Finish with 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
9. Serve hot.

## Notes
- Take the onion mixture to a proper golden brown before adding the tomatoes; that gives the curry depth and keeps the gravy from tasting sharp.
- Mushrooms release liquid as they cook, so keep the simmer steady rather than high. Uncover at the end only if you want a thicker finish.
- Taste after the mushrooms are cooked through. If the tomato still tastes bright, give it another minute or two before serving.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently so the mushrooms stay tender.
- This curry freezes well, though the mushrooms will soften a little after thawing.
