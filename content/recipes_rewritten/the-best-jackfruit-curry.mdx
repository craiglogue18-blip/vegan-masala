## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil, or oil of choice
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds
- 1 1/2 teaspoons black mustard seeds, or brown mustard seeds
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece fresh ginger, minced or grated
- 1 serrano pepper, diced
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon coriander
- 1 teaspoon sweet or hot paprika
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
- 6 to 8 dried curry leaves, or 3 to 4 fresh curry leaves
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 3/4 cup water, or 1 cup water for stovetop version
- 1 (13.5-ounce / 400 mL) can full-fat coconut milk
- 2 (20-ounce / 565g) cans jackfruit, drained and rinsed
- 3 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1 (8-ounce / 225g) can tomato sauce
- 4 to 5 cups baby kale or baby spinach, roughly chopped
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice or lime juice
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems, chopped
- White basmati rice or flatbread, for serving

## Method
1. Drain and rinse the 2 cans jackfruit well. Pull the pieces apart with your hands and discard any tough cores.
2. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté and heat 1 tablespoon coconut oil. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds and 1 1/2 teaspoons black mustard seeds, and cook for about 45 seconds, until they start to pop.
3. Add 1 large yellow onion, diced, and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring often, until softened and lightly golden.
4. Stir in 6 cloves garlic, minced, 2-inch piece fresh ginger, minced or grated, and 1 serrano pepper, diced. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring so the garlic does not catch.
5. Add 2 teaspoons garam masala, 1 teaspoon ground turmeric, 1 teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon sweet or hot paprika, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder, 6 to 8 dried curry leaves, and 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt. Stir for 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
6. Pour in 3/4 cup water and scrape the base of the pot well, loosening any browned bits.
7. Add 1 (13.5-ounce / 400 mL) can full-fat coconut milk, the jackfruit, and 3 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and chopped. Stir to combine, then cancel Sauté.
8. Seal the lid and pressure cook on high for 6 minutes.
9. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then vent the remaining steam.
10. Open the pot and stir in 1 (8-ounce / 225g) can tomato sauce and 4 to 5 cups baby kale or baby spinach, roughly chopped. Set the pot to Sauté and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes, until the greens wilt and the sauce thickens slightly.
11. Stir in 1 tablespoon lemon juice or lime juice and 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems, chopped. Taste and adjust the salt before serving.
12. For stovetop cooking, follow the same order in a heavy pot, using 1 cup water instead of 3/4 cup water, then simmer covered until the sweet potatoes are tender and the jackfruit has absorbed the masala.

## Notes
- The curry thickens as it sits. For a more cohesive sauce, mash a few pieces of sweet potato against the side of the pot before serving.
- If the sauce is too thick after reheating, loosen it with a splash of water. Warm it gently so the coconut milk stays smooth.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. The seasoning settles overnight, and the jackfruit takes on more of the masala.
- If the jackfruit is still firm after cooking, give it a little more time on the stovetop or add a small splash of water before pressure cooking next time.
