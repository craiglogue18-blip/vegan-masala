## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 bay leaf (optional)
- 1 small cinnamon stick (optional)
- 3 cloves (optional)
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 5–6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 cups tomatoes, chopped
- 15 cashews
- 1 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder or paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 cup water (for blending)
- 2 tbsp vegan butter
- 250–300g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp sugar or jaggery (optional)
- ½ tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)
- 3–4 tbsp plant cream or coconut cream
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 2 green cardamom pods, 1 bay leaf if using, 1 small cinnamon stick if using, and 3 cloves if using.
2. Add 1 medium onion, sliced, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges.
3. Stir in 5–6 garlic cloves, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 minute, stirring so the raw smell cooks off.
4. Add 2 cups tomatoes, chopped, 15 cashews, and 1 tsp salt. Cook until the tomatoes break down and the masala turns thick and glossy.
5. Stir in 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder or paprika, 1 tsp ground coriander, and 1 tsp garam masala. Cook for 30 seconds, then take the pan off the heat.
6. Blend the cooked masala with 1 cup water until completely smooth.
7. Melt 2 tbsp vegan butter in the same pan. Pour in the blended sauce and simmer gently until it thickens a little and no raw tomato taste remains.
8. Add 250–300g firm tofu, cubed, and simmer for 4 to 5 minutes so it warms through without breaking up.
9. Stir in 1 tsp sugar or jaggery if using, ½ tbsp kasuri methi, and 3–4 tbsp plant cream or coconut cream. Cook for 1 minute more, then switch off the heat.
10. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take time with the onion-tomato base; that is where the sauce gets its depth and body. If the tomatoes still taste sharp before blending, cook them a few minutes longer.
- Blend until very smooth for a classic butter masala texture. If the curry thickens too much after simmering, loosen it with a splash of hot water.
- Add the tofu near the end so it stays soft and holds its shape. Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer rather than letting it boil hard.
- Taste after the cream and kasuri methi go in, then adjust salt or sweetness if needed. The sauce settles quickly once the heat goes off.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days in a covered container. Reheat over low heat with a little water and stir gently so the sauce stays even.
- The curry thickens as it sits, and the spices mellow by the next day. Save a little hot water when reheating if you want a looser gravy.
