## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 200 g spinach
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook slowly until soft and golden at the edges.
3. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, along with 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1 tsp chilli powder. Cook until the tomato breaks down and the masala looks thick and glossy.
5. Add 3 medium potatoes, cubed, and turn them through the masala until coated well.
6. Pour in 1 cup water, cover, and cook on a low simmer until the potatoes are tender and most of the water has reduced.
7. Fold in 200 g spinach and cook just until wilted.
8. Season with 1 tsp salt, stir well, and cook for 1 minute more so the seasoning settles into the potatoes.

## Notes
- Take the onions a little further than softening alone; the light colour helps build a deeper masala base.
- If the tomato-spice mixture starts sticking, add a small splash of water and keep stirring. The masala should fry down, not scorch.
- Cut the potatoes into even cubes so they cook at the same pace and hold their shape at the end.
- Add the spinach at the end and cook briefly. Overcooking turns it dull and watery.
- The dish thickens as it stands. When reheating, add a splash of water and warm it gently over low heat.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days. The flavour settles nicely overnight.
