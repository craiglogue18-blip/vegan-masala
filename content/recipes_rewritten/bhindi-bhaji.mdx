## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 8 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 15g/½oz fresh root ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
- 500g/1lb 2oz bhindi (okra), trimmed and cut into 4cm/1½in slices
- 2–3 green chillies (bird’s eye or similar), sliced in half lengthways
- 2 tsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp amchoor (mango powder)
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 175ml/6fl oz coconut milk

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp sunflower oil in a large wok or frying pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds, then stir in 8 sliced garlic cloves and 15g sliced ginger. Cook for about 1 minute, just until fragrant and lightly softened.
2. Add 500g okra and 2–3 halved green chillies. Stir well so the okra is coated in the oil and spices, then cook for 8 minutes, stirring now and then, until it begins to soften.
3. Stir in 2 tsp tomato purée, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp amchoor and 2 tsp ground coriander. Cook for 2 minutes, letting the tomato purée lose its raw edge and the spices coat the okra.
4. Pour in 175ml coconut milk and simmer for about 8 minutes, until the bhindi is tender but still holding its shape. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed, then serve hot.

## Notes
- Okra likes steady heat and a light hand. Keep stirring to a minimum once it starts softening, so the slices hold their shape instead of breaking down.
- If the coconut milk looks a little thin at first, let it reduce gently rather than turning the heat up high. The sauce should sit around the okra, not drown it.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat slowly in a pan so the okra stays intact; it is better than microwaving for this dish.
- Serve it soon after cooking for the best texture, with the bhindi still tender and the sauce lightly coating each piece.
