## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup Instant Oats
- 1/2 cup Sooji (semolina)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened plant-based yogurt
- 1/2 cup Water, plus extra as needed
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp Cooking Oil
- 1/2 tsp Mustard Seeds
- 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
- 1 tsp Chana Dal
- A few Curry Leaves, fresh or dried
- 1/2 tsp Baking Soda
- Cooking Oil, for greasing the idli stand

## Method
1. In a large bowl, combine 1/2 cup instant oats, 1/2 cup sooji, 1/2 cup unsweetened plant-based yogurt, 1/2 cup water, and salt to taste. Mix well and rest for 30 minutes so the sooji softens and the batter thickens.
2. Check the batter after resting. Add a little more water only if needed; it should be thicker than dosa batter but still pourable.
3. Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds, and 1 tsp chana dal.
4. Let the seeds splutter and the chana dal turn light golden, then add a few curry leaves and stir for a few seconds.
5. Pour the tempering into the batter and mix well.
6. Just before steaming, sprinkle in 1/2 tsp baking soda and fold it through gently. Do not overmix.
7. Bring about 1 cup water to a boil in a deep pan. Grease each cavity of the idli stand with cooking oil.
8. Fill each mould halfway with batter, then place the stand in the pan and cover tightly.
9. Steam on low heat for 10 to 15 minutes, until the idlis are set and a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
10. Turn off the heat and rest the idlis for 2 to 3 minutes before removing them from the moulds.
11. Serve warm with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Notes
- The batter should stay thick enough to hold shape in the moulds; if it gets too loose, the idlis turn dense.
- Add the baking soda only at the end, just before steaming, so the batter keeps its lift.
- These idlis are best steamed and served straight away, but leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 2 days.
- Reheat in a steamer or in the microwave with a few drops of water so they soften again instead of drying out.
- Grated carrot, chopped spinach, or a little finely chopped coriander can be mixed into the batter if you want more texture and colour.
