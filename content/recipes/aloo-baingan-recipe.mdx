## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 medium onions, chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 2 medium eggplants, sliced lengthwise
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Keep 3 medium cubed potatoes in water until needed. Soak 2 medium eggplants, sliced lengthwise, in water with a pinch of the 1 tsp salt to slow browning.
2. Heat 2 tbsp vegetable oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
3. Add 2 medium chopped onions and 1 green chilli, finely chopped. Cook until the onions turn soft and lightly golden.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and cook for 30 to 40 seconds, just until fragrant.
5. Drain the potatoes and add them to the pan. Stir-fry for 3 minutes so the edges begin to turn opaque.
6. Add the eggplant slices and cook for a few minutes, stirring gently, until they start to soften.
7. Sprinkle in 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 2 tsp coriander powder, the remaining salt and 1 red chilli, finely chopped. Mix well so the vegetables are coated in the masala.
8. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down and the raw smell cooks off.
9. Pour in 1 cup water, cover, and simmer until the potatoes are tender and the eggplant is soft. Add a little more hot water if the pan dries out before the vegetables are cooked.
10. Uncover and cook for a few minutes more if needed, until the sauce looks settled and lightly thickened. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Let the onions cook properly before adding the potatoes. That base gives the curry body and helps the spices cling to the vegetables.
- If the masala starts sticking, lower the heat and add a splash of water instead of letting it scorch.
- Cut the potatoes and eggplant evenly so they finish cooking at roughly the same time.
- For a drier curry, simmer uncovered at the end. For more gravy, add a little more hot water and simmer gently.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat with a spoonful of water so the sauce loosens again.
