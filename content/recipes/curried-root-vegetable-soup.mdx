## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp rapeseed oil
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp ginger root, grated
- 1 tbsp korma curry powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp coriander
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 300 g swede, diced (cut smaller for even cooking)
- 300 g parsnips, diced
- 300 g carrots, diced
- 200 g potatoes, diced
- 1.2 L vegetable stock
- Freshly ground salt, to taste
- Freshly ground pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat 4 tbsp rapeseed oil in a large pan over a gentle heat. Add the 2 diced onions, 4 crushed garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger, then cover and sweat for about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onions are soft.
2. Raise the heat slightly and stir in 1 tbsp korma curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander and ½ tsp garam masala. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring so the spices bloom in the oil.
3. Add 300 g swede, 300 g parsnips, 300 g carrots and 200 g potatoes. Stir for a few minutes so the vegetables are well coated in the masala.
4. Pour in 1.2 L vegetable stock, then season with freshly ground salt and freshly ground pepper to taste. Bring to a boil, lower to a simmer, cover and cook for 25-30 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the swede breaks down easily when pressed against the pan.
5. Blend the soup to your preferred texture. Leave a little body if you want a more rustic finish, then taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
6. Reheat gently before serving, if required.

## Notes
- The soup keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat slowly on the hob and loosen with a little water if it thickens after chilling.
- A partial blend gives a thicker, more textured bowl. Blend half the soup and leave the rest chunky if you want more bite from the vegetables.
- If the soup tastes flat at the end, add a little more salt rather than more spice; the vegetable sweetness should still come through.
- Freeze in portions once cool. Defrost fully before reheating so the texture stays even.
