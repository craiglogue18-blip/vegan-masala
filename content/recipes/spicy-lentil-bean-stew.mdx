## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons oil
- 1½ cups chopped onion
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 black cardamom pod, partially opened, optional
- ¼ teaspoon crushed fennel seeds
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder (or paprika)
- ½ teaspoon ground cardamom
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon (scant measure)
- 1/8 teaspoon ground clove
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon garam masala (or curry powder)
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste (or 5 cloves garlic minced and half an inch ginger minced)
- 1 cup chopped tomato (or ½ cup tomato purée or Passata)
- ½ teaspoon salt, divided
- 15 oz can full-fat coconut milk
- 1½ cups water or stock, divided
- ¼ to ⅓ cup split red lentils
- 15 oz can white beans
- ½ cup frozen spinach, thawed, or 2 oz fresh chopped spinach
- cilantro, lemon juice, red pepper flakes, for garnish
- non-dairy cream or yogurt, for garnish, optional

## Method
1. In a small bowl, mix 1 teaspoon black pepper, 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, ½ teaspoon ground cardamom, ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon, 1/8 teaspoon ground clove, 2 teaspoons ground coriander, and 1 teaspoon garam masala. Keep this spice blend aside.
2. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Add 1½ cups chopped onion with a pinch of the ½ teaspoon salt and cook for about 3 minutes, until the onions turn translucent.
3. Add 1 bay leaf, 1 black cardamom pod if using, and ¼ teaspoon crushed fennel seeds. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes more, adding 2 tablespoons of the water or stock if the pan starts to catch and the onions need more time to soften.
4. When the onions are lightly golden, add three-quarters of the spice blend with 2 tablespoons of the water or stock. Stir for 10 to 15 seconds, then add 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste and 1 cup chopped tomato. Cook until the tomatoes break down and turn jammy, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. Pour in 15 oz coconut milk, the remaining water or stock, the remaining salt, ¼ to ⅓ cup split red lentils, and 15 oz white beans. Stir well, partly cover, and simmer for 12 to 15 minutes, until the lentils are soft and the stew has a creamy body.
6. Stir in ½ cup thawed frozen spinach and cook for 1 minute, just until heated through. Taste and adjust the salt, then add a little lemon juice for lift.
7. Turn off the heat and sprinkle the remaining spice blend over the top. Finish with cilantro, red pepper flakes, a last squeeze of lemon, and non-dairy cream or yogurt if you like.

## Notes
- The stew thickens as it sits because of the lentils and beans. If you want it looser, keep extra water or stock nearby and add a splash while reheating.
- For more heat, use the full amount of Kashmiri chili powder and finish with red pepper flakes. For a milder pot, use paprika and hold back a little of the spice blend at the end.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat so the coconut milk stays smooth; avoid a hard boil.
- If you use tomato purée instead of chopped tomato, cook it down well with the masala so the raw edge disappears before adding the liquid.
