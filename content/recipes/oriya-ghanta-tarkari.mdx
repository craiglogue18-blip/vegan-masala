## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 600 g mixed seasonal vegetables, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large finely chopped onion and 1 green chilli, then cook until the onion turns soft and translucent.
3. Stir in 4 minced garlic cloves and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, just until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 2 tsp coriander powder and 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, then stir for a few seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add 600 g mixed seasonal vegetables and 1 tsp salt. Stir well and cook for a few minutes until the vegetables are coated in the masala.
6. Add 200 g firm tofu cubes with a splash of water if the pan looks dry. Cover and simmer until the vegetables are tender and the tofu has taken on the masala.
7. Finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed, then serve warm.

## Notes
- Cook the onion properly before adding the spices; that base gives the dish its body and keeps the masala from tasting flat.
- Season after the vegetables have softened a little, then taste again at the end because tofu and vegetables both soften the salt level.
- If the pan dries out before the vegetables are tender, add water a tablespoon at a time rather than flooding it.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat best over low heat with a small splash of water.
- The texture should stay fairly dry and spoonable, not soupy. Let the vegetables hold their shape.
