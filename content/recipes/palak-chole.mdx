## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 200 g spinach, chopped
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves

## Method
1. Drain and rinse 1 tin chickpeas and set them aside.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden.
3. Stir in 4 garlic cloves, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for a minute until the raw smell eases.
4. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, and cook until it breaks down and the masala looks glossy and the oil begins to show at the edges.
5. Add 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, and 1 tsp salt. Cook for 30 to 60 seconds, stirring so the spices do not catch.
6. Add 200 g spinach, chopped, and cook until wilted and well mixed through the masala.
7. Stir in 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed, and 1 cup water. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until the gravy thickens.
8. Mash a small handful of the chickpeas against the side of the pan to give the curry more body.
9. Turn off the heat. Stir in 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder), and 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves.
10. Taste and adjust salt or amchur if needed, then serve hot.

## Notes
- The onion and tomato need real cooking time here; a well-cooked masala gives the curry its depth and body.
- If the gravy looks too thick, loosen it with a little hot water. If it is too thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes longer.
- Chickpeas break down enough to thicken the sauce, but a few whole ones should stay intact for texture.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and often taste better the next day.
- Reheat gently with a splash of water so the spinach stays soft and the gravy does not dry out.
