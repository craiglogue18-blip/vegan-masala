## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Boil the 3 medium potatoes, cubed, until tender, then drain and peel if needed.
2. Heat 2 tbsp vegetable oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp mustard seeds and let them crackle.
3. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, then tip in the boiled potatoes and 1 tsp salt.
4. Sprinkle over 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp chilli powder, 2 tsp coriander powder and 1 tsp garam masala.
5. Stir gently so the potatoes are coated, then cook for 5 to 7 minutes, letting the masala cling to the edges. Add a small splash of water if the pan looks dry or the spices begin to catch.
6. Turn off the heat and finish with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Serve warm.

## Notes
- For a drier finish, let the potatoes sit in the pan for the last few minutes without stirring too often so the masala can stick and lightly toast.
- If your potatoes are very soft, handle them carefully so they stay in pieces rather than turning mushy.
- A splash of water is enough if the pan starts to catch; too much will wash the spices off the potatoes.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat best in a pan over low heat.
- The flavour settles overnight, so this is one of those dishes that can taste even better the next day.
