## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp salt
- coconut sugar (optional)
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp coconut oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add 1 large onion, chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden.
2. Stir in 3 garlic cloves, minced, and 1-inch piece of ginger, grated. Cook for 30 seconds, until the raw edge fades.
3. Add 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garam masala, and 1 tsp ground cumin. Stir for a minute so the spices bloom in the oil.
4. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down into a thick masala and the oil starts to show at the edges.
5. Stir in 1 cup red lentils, rinsed, and 4 cups vegetable broth. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then.
6. Add 1 tin coconut milk and 1 tsp salt. Simmer until the lentils are very soft and the dal has a creamy, spoonable body.
7. Taste and adjust with coconut sugar, if needed, and 1 tbsp lemon juice. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion and tomatoes properly; that base gives the dal its body and keeps the spices from tasting flat.
- Red lentils soften quickly, so keep the simmer gentle and stir often enough to stop them catching on the bottom.
- Add the lemon juice at the end so the dal stays round rather than sharp.
- Leftovers thicken in the fridge. Loosen with a splash of water or broth when reheating.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
