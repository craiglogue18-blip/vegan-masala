## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 8 large garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 15g/½oz fresh root ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
- 500g/1lb 2oz bhindi (okra), trimmed and cut into 4cm/1½in slices
- 2–3 green chillies (bird’s eye or similar), sliced in half lengthways
- 2 tsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp amchoor (mango powder)
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 175ml/6fl oz coconut milk

## Method
1. In a large wok or frying pan, heat 2 tablespoons of sunflower oil over medium heat. Once hot, add 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds; when they sizzle, add 8 thinly sliced garlic cloves and 15g of thinly sliced ginger. Saute for 1 minute.
2. Next, add 500g of prepared bhindi (okra) and 2–3 halved green chillies. Stir and cook for 8 minutes, allowing the okra to soften.
3. Incorporate 2 teaspoons of tomato purée, 1 teaspoon of turmeric, 1 teaspoon of salt, 1 teaspoon of amchoor, and 2 teaspoons of ground coriander. Stir well and cook for another 2 minutes.
4. Finally, stir in 175ml of coconut milk and simmer for 8 more minutes, until the bhindi is tender yet retains its bite. Serve hot.

## Notes
- For the best texture, avoid overcooking the okra; it should remain slightly firm. Leftovers can be stored in the fridge for up to three days and reheated gently on the stovetop.
- Pair this dish with steamed rice or flatbreads to soak up the delicious sauce.
