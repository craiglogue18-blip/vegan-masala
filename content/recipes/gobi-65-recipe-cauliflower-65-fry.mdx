## Ingredients
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 cup water
- 500 ml neutral oil for deep-frying, plus 2 tbsp for the masala
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- a handful of curry leaves
- 2 tsp chilli powder, divided
- 2 tsp garam masala, divided
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1/2 cup plain flour
- 1 cup rice flour
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Blanch the cauliflower florets in hot water for 2 to 3 minutes, then drain and rinse quickly. Let them dry well so the batter clings properly.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine 1/2 cup plain flour, 1 cup rice flour, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp salt, and 1 cup water. Stir to a thick batter that coats the back of a spoon.
3. Add the cauliflower florets to the batter and coat them evenly.
4. Deep fry the battered florets in 500 ml hot oil until crisp and golden. Drain well on a rack or paper towel.
5. Heat the remaining 2 tbsp oil in a pan. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them crackle, then add 4 cloves minced garlic, 2 finely chopped green chillies, and a handful of curry leaves.
6. Stir in the remaining 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala, and 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt. Cook for a minute or two until the masala loosens, then thickens slightly and looks cooked through.
7. Add the fried cauliflower and toss over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes until the masala coats the florets without softening the crust too much.
8. Serve immediately while the edges are still crisp.

## Notes
- Dry the cauliflower well after blanching; extra moisture makes the batter heavy and the coating less crisp.
- The yoghurt needs a brief cook so it loses the raw edge and settles into the masala. Keep the heat steady and do not rush that step.
- If the batter feels too thick after resting, loosen it with a spoon or two of water. It should cling without running off.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days, though the coating softens. Reheat in a hot pan or air fryer rather than the microwave.
- Adjust salt and chilli only after the final toss, once the masala has coated the cauliflower properly.
