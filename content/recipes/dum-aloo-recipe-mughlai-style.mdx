## Ingredients
- 3 medium potatoes
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 3 cloves
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tsp shahi jeera (caraway seeds)
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Boil the 3 medium potatoes until just tender. Drain, cool slightly, peel if needed, and prick them with a fork so they take in the masala.
2. Blend 1 large onion, chopped, 1 inch ginger, grated, 3 garlic cloves, and 1 tsp fennel seeds with a little water to a smooth paste.
3. Stir 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt into the paste and blend briefly until even.
4. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a heavy pan. Add 1 tsp shahi jeera, 1 small cinnamon stick, 3 cloves, 2 green cardamom pods, and 1 bay leaf. Let them sizzle for a few seconds.
5. Add the onion paste and cook on low, stirring often, until the raw smell goes and the masala turns thick, darker, and glossy.
6. Add 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala, and 1 tsp salt. Stir for a minute so the spices bloom in the masala.
7. Add the potatoes and turn them gently so they are coated all over.
8. Pour in 1 cup water, bring it to a gentle simmer, then cover tightly and cook on low for 10 to 12 minutes.
9. Uncover and cook for a minute or two if needed to thicken the gravy slightly. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and rest for a few minutes before serving.

## Notes
- Cook the onion masala slowly. If it stays pale, the gravy will taste raw; if it darkens too fast, lower the heat and add a spoonful of water.
- Pricking the potatoes helps the masala move into the flesh, which matters more here than a thick coating on the outside.
- This curry settles as it sits. The potatoes soften a little more and the gravy clings better after a short rest.
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of water so the gravy loosens without breaking.
- If you want a looser gravy, add a little more water near the end and simmer uncovered for a minute.
