## Ingredients
- 3 medium potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 cup chickpea flour (besan)
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 500 ml neutral oil, for deep-frying

## Method
1. Peel and thinly slice the 3 medium potatoes.
2. In a large bowl, combine 1 cup chickpea flour (besan), 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon red chilli powder, 1 teaspoon garam masala, and 1 tsp salt.
3. Pour in 1 cup water gradually, stirring until you have a thick batter that coats the back of a spoon.
4. Stir the sliced potatoes into the batter so each slice is well covered.
5. Heat 500 ml neutral oil in a deep, heavy pan to 175°C.
6. Drop in the battered potatoes in batches and fry until golden brown and crisp on both sides.
7. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper.

## Notes
- Slice the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same rate; very thick slices stay underdone while the batter darkens too fast.
- Keep the batter thick enough to cling to the potatoes. If it loosens after standing, add a spoonful of besan rather than more water.
- Hold the heat at medium so the spices cook through without turning bitter.
- These are best eaten soon after frying, while the coating is still crisp.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a hot oven or air fryer so the coating firms up again.
