## Ingredients
- 1/4 of a small yellow onion, coarsely chopped
- 2-3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1-inch piece of fresh ginger, chopped
- 2-8 green chilies, chopped (2 for mild, 4-8 for spicy)
- 1 bunch cilantro, washed and cut into 1-2 inch sections, including the stems
- 1/2 cup fresh mint, coarsely chopped
- 1/2 cup vegan plain unsweetened yogurt
- 1/2-1 tsp salt, to taste
- 2-4 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon or lime juice, to taste

## Method
1. In a food processor or blender, add 1/4 of a small yellow onion, 2-3 chopped cloves garlic, 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, and 2-8 chopped green chilies. Process until the mixture is finely minced.
2. Add 1 bunch cilantro, 1/2 cup fresh mint, 1/2 cup vegan plain unsweetened yogurt, 1/2-1 tsp salt, and 2-4 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon or lime juice. Blend until smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides as needed; keep a little texture if you like it that way.
3. Taste and adjust with more salt or lemon or lime juice if needed. If the chutney seems too thick, blend in a spoonful of water at a time until it loosens slightly.

## Notes
- This chutney is best made close to serving, since the herbs lose some brightness after a day or two. It keeps well in a covered container in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.
- For longer storage, freeze in small portions and thaw in the fridge. Stir before serving, since the yogurt can separate a little after chilling.
- Use more green chillies for heat, or keep them low and let the ginger and lemon carry the sharpness. A thicker chutney works well as a spread; a looser one suits dipping.
