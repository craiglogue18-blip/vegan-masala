## Ingredients
- 1 cup moong dal, rinsed
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Add 1 cup rinsed moong dal, 1 large finely chopped onion, 2 medium chopped tomatoes, 1 inch grated ginger, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp salt, and 1 cup water to the pressure cooker.
2. Seal and cook on High Pressure for 20 minutes.
3. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then release any remaining pressure.
4. Open the lid and stir the dal well so the lentils break down a little and the texture looks even.
5. Stir in 1 tsp sugar and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
6. Taste and adjust salt or lemon if needed.
7. Finish with 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander and serve hot.

## Notes
- The dal should be soft and lightly broken, with enough body to coat a spoon. If it looks too thick after pressure cooking, loosen it with a little hot water.
- If the tomatoes still taste sharp, give the dal a few minutes off the heat before serving. The flavour settles as it stands.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat cleanly on the stove with a splash of water.
- For a thicker bowl, simmer briefly after pressure cooking; for a looser dal, add hot water a little at a time until it reaches the texture you want.
