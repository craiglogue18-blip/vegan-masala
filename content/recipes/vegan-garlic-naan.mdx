## Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1.5 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 cup unsweetened soy yoghurt
- 0.25 cup unsweetened soy milk
- 1.5 Tbsp unsalted vegan butter
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. In a large bowl, combine 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1.5 Tbsp granulated sugar, 1 tsp baking powder, and 0.5 tsp salt.
2. Add 0.5 cup unsweetened soy yoghurt and 0.25 cup unsweetened soy milk, then mix until a soft dough forms.
3. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead for a few minutes until smooth and supple.
4. Divide the dough into equal portions and shape each piece into a ball.
5. Roll each ball into an oval about 0.5 cm thick.
6. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and cook each naan for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until spotted and lightly golden.
7. Melt 1.5 Tbsp unsalted vegan butter, then stir in 2 minced garlic cloves and set aside.
8. Brush the hot naan with the garlic butter and finish with 0.5 cup fresh coriander, chopped.

## Notes
- Keep the dough soft; too much extra flour will make the naan dry and stiff.
- Cook over steady medium heat so the bread cooks through without scorching before the centre sets.
- Brush with the garlic butter as soon as the naan comes off the pan so it soaks in properly.
- These are best served fresh, but leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat well in a hot pan.
- If the dough feels tight, add a teaspoon of soy milk at a time until it loosens.
