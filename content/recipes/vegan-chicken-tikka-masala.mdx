## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil (sunflower, vegetable)
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- A thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon chili powder (optional)
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1 teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves (methi, optional)
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 tablespoon tomato puree
- 1 x 400 g tin of finely chopped tomatoes or passata
- 1 x 400 g tin of coconut milk
- 320 g plant-based chick’n (any brand)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- A small handful of coriander, very finely chopped
- Salt

## Method
1. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large, heavy-based saucepan. Add 1 finely diced large onion and a pinch of salt, sautéing on low heat for about 10 minutes until the onion is soft, translucent, and starting to brown slightly.
2. Stir in 4 grated garlic cloves and a grated thumb-sized piece of ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add 2 teaspoons of garam masala, 1 teaspoon of chili powder (if using), 1 teaspoon of cumin, 1 teaspoon of ground cardamom, 1 teaspoon of dried fenugreek leaves (if using), and 1 teaspoon of turmeric. Incorporate a splash of water to prevent the spices from scorching, cooking for another minute to bloom the spices.
4. Mix in 1 tablespoon of tomato puree and cook for an additional minute before adding 1 tin of finely chopped tomatoes, 1 tin of coconut milk, and 320 g of plant-based chick’n.
5. Simmer on medium-low heat for 20 minutes, or until the sauce thickens and becomes creamy. Season to taste with salt and the juice of 1/2 lemon.
6. Serve garnished with a small handful of finely chopped coriander.

## Notes
- This masala sauce thickens over time, making it ideal for leftovers; simply reheat gently on the stove, adding a splash of water if too thick. Adjust seasoning as needed before serving.
- The texture of the chick’n will remain tender, and the spices will deepen in flavor if allowed to sit for a while.
- For a creamier sauce, you can add a bit more coconut milk.
