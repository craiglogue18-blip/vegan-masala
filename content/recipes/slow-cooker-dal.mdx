## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- thumb of fresh root ginger, finely grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 garlic cloves, one crushed and one thinly sliced
- 300g yellow split peas
- 200g chopped tomatoes (from a tin or fresh)
- 10 freeze-dried curry leaves
- 700ml hot vegetable stock
- 1 hot green finger chilli, thinly sliced
- salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- lemon wedges, to serve

## Method
1. Put the 300g yellow split peas, 1 chopped onion, 200g chopped tomatoes, thumb of grated ginger, 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground turmeric, 1 crushed garlic clove, 10 freeze-dried curry leaves and 700ml hot vegetable stock into the slow cooker. Add most of the 1 thinly sliced green chilli and stir well.
2. Cook on high for 4 hours, or until the split peas are fully tender and the dal has broken down into a thick, spoonable consistency. Stir once or twice if you can.
3. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper. If the dal looks too loose, leave it uncovered for a few minutes to reduce slightly; if it is very thick, loosen with a splash of hot water.
4. For the tempering, heat 2 tbsp vegetable or sunflower oil in a small saucepan until hot. Add the remaining 1 sliced garlic clove and fry until golden, then tip it over the dal.
5. Finish with the remaining green chilli and serve at once with lemon wedges for squeezing.

## Notes
- The dal thickens as it stands, so add a little hot water when reheating if needed. Reheat gently on the hob or in the microwave until steaming hot.
- For a smoother finish, blend part or all of the cooked dal before serving. Leave some texture if you want it more rustic.
- Freeze in portions for up to 2 months. Thaw fully before reheating and stir well, as the split peas settle and thicken in storage.
- If you want more heat, keep some chilli back for the end; if you prefer a milder bowl, use only half the chilli in the cooker.
- Fresh coriander is a good last-minute addition, but the dal does not need much else beyond rice, a squeeze of lemon and something crisp alongside.
