## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups potatoes (cubed, about 3 medium)
- ¼ cup green peas (optional)
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 sprig curry leaves (optional)
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
- 2 green chilies (chopped)
- 1 teaspoon ginger paste
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons coriander leaves (finely chopped)
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 6 slices bread
- ¾ cup gram flour (besan, can use coarse for added texture)
- 2 to 3 tablespoons rice flour (or cornstarch)
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder
- ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ to ¾ teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain or vaamu)
- Water (as needed)
- oil (as needed for shallow or deep frying)

## Method
1. Steam or boil 1 ½ cups potatoes until just tender. If using, cook the ¼ cup green peas with them, then drain well.
2. Peel and mash the potatoes in a bowl. Keep the mash fairly coarse so the filling holds its shape.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a pan. Add 1 sprig curry leaves, 1 pinch asafoetida, 2 green chilies, and 1 teaspoon ginger paste. Cook briefly until fragrant.
4. Add ½ teaspoon red chili powder, ½ teaspoon garam masala, and ⅛ teaspoon turmeric. Stir for a few seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add the mashed potatoes, ¼ cup green peas, ½ teaspoon salt, and 3 tablespoons coriander leaves. Mix well, then finish with 1 tablespoon lemon juice and adjust the seasoning if needed.
6. Lay out 6 slices bread. Spread the potato filling on 3 slices, cover with the remaining slices, press lightly, and cut each sandwich into triangles if you like.
7. In a bowl, mix ¾ cup gram flour, 2 to 3 tablespoons rice flour, ½ teaspoon red chili powder, ⅛ teaspoon turmeric, ½ teaspoon salt, and ½ to ¾ teaspoon carom seeds.
8. Add water gradually to make a thick, smooth batter that coats the bread without running off too quickly.
9. Heat oil in a pan for shallow or deep frying. Test a little batter first; it should rise steadily without browning at once.
10. Dip each bread sandwich in the batter, let the excess drip off, and slide it into the hot oil. Fry a few at a time without crowding the pan.
11. Cook on medium heat until one side is golden and crisp, then turn and fry the other side. Drain on a rack or paper.
12. For air frying, make the batter slightly thicker, coat the sandwiches, and place them on parchment. Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8 minutes, turn carefully, then air fry for 6 to 7 minutes more until crisp and browned.

## Notes
- A slightly coarse potato mash gives the filling better body and keeps the sandwiches from turning pasty. If the filling feels wet, cook it a minute longer before assembling.
- The batter should cling in a thin, even layer. If it is too thick, the coating can feel doughy; too thin and it will not fry up well.
- These are best eaten hot, when the bread is still crisp at the edges. They can be reheated in an air fryer or oven, though the coating will soften a little after cooling.
- Keep leftover filling covered in the fridge for 1 to 2 days. Assemble and fry only when you are ready to serve.
- Adjust the chilli to suit the bread and filling you use; plain white bread usually benefits from a little more seasoning in the potato mixture.
