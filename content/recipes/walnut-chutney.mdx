## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp cooking oil, divided
- 1 cup raw walnuts
- 1/3 cup black gram lentils (split and skinned)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 7-8 green chilies or 2-3 Serrano peppers
- 1/2 inch ginger, peeled and cut
- 3/4 cup plain unsweetened vegan yogurt
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp frozen shredded coconut (optional)
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 2-4 dried red chilies
- Few curry leaves (optional)
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper powder

## Method
1. Heat 1/2 tbsp cooking oil in a cast-iron skillet or pan over medium heat. Add 1 cup raw walnuts and roast until they smell nutty and take on a light colour.
2. Add 1/3 cup black gram lentils and 1 tsp cumin seeds. Keep stirring until the lentils are lightly browned and the cumin is fragrant, then turn off the heat and let the mixture cool slightly.
3. Transfer the cooled walnut-lentil mixture to a blender. Add 7-8 green chilies or 2-3 Serrano peppers, 1/2 inch ginger, 3/4 cup vegan yogurt, salt to taste, and 1 tbsp frozen shredded coconut if using.
4. Blend to a smooth chutney, adding a little more water only if needed for the blade to move. Taste and adjust salt or green chilies if you want more heat.
5. Pour the chutney into a serving bowl.
6. In the same pan, heat the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil; add 1 tsp black mustard seeds. When they splutter, add 2-4 dried red chilies and few curry leaves, if using.
7. Once the red chilies darken, stir in 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper powder and turn off the heat.
8. Spoon the tempering over the chutney and mix lightly before serving.

## Notes
- The chutney thickens as it rests. Stir in a splash of water before serving if you want a looser texture.
- Keep it in a covered container in the fridge for up to 5 days. The flavour settles after a few hours, so it is often better the next day.
- Use fewer green chilies for a milder chutney, or keep the seed membranes in for more heat.
- The optional coconut softens the edge of the walnuts and gives the chutney a rounder finish, but it is not needed for a good result.
