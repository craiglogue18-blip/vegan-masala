## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups raw basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes, preferably)
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 1/4 cups water
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp avocado oil or any neutral oil
- 1 tbsp chana dal (Bengal gram dal)
- 2 tsp urad dal (Black gram dal)
- 2 dry red chili peppers (or 1 tsp red pepper flakes; adjust to preference)
- 1 tbsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 cup raw cashews (chopped coarsely)
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 3/4 cup shredded coconut (unsweetened)

## Method
1. Drain the soaked 1.5 cups basmati rice and add it to a saucepan with 1 cup coconut milk, 1 1/4 cups water, and salt to taste.
2. Bring to a boil, then cover with a tight-fitting lid, lower the heat, and cook for 15 minutes until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
3. While the rice cooks, heat 1/2 tablespoon oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add 1 tablespoon chana dal, 1 teaspoon urad dal, and 2 dry red chili peppers, and toast until the dals turn golden.
4. Grind or crush the toasted chana dal, urad dal, and chilies to a coarse powder; set aside.
5. In the same skillet, heat the remaining 1/2 tablespoon oil. Add 1 tablespoon mustard seeds and let them splutter.
6. Stir in the remaining 1 teaspoon urad dal, 1/4 cup chopped cashews, and 1 sprig curry leaves. Cook until the urad dal and cashews are golden and the curry leaves look crisp.
7. Add 3/4 cup shredded coconut and sauté briefly until it turns lightly golden and smells nutty.
8. Fold the coconut mixture and the ground dal powder through the rice with a fork, taking care not to break the grains. Taste and add more salt only if needed, then serve warm.

## Notes
- Soaking the rice helps it cook evenly and stay separate. If you skip the soak, add a little extra water and watch the pot closely near the end.
- The coconut should only be toasted lightly. If it browns too quickly, it can turn dry and bitter.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a spoon of water or a splash of coconut milk to loosen the grains.
- This rice holds its texture well beside a dry vegetable stir-fry or a saucy lentil dish. A few fresh curry leaves or chopped coriander on top is enough.
- If using red pepper flakes instead of dry chilies, add them with the mustard seeds so they do not scorch.
