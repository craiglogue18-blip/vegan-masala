## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp mustard seeds and 1 tsp cumin seeds, and let them crackle.
2. Add 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Stir for 20 to 30 seconds until the raw smell eases.
3. Stir in 1 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder and 2 tsp coriander powder. Cook briefly so the spices bloom in the oil.
4. Add 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets, and 3 medium potatoes, cubed. Turn them through the masala until the surface is well coated.
5. Pour in 1 cup water and add 1 tsp salt. Cover and cook on medium heat until the potatoes are tender and the cauliflower is soft but still holding its shape.
6. Uncover and simmer for a few minutes if needed to reduce the liquid slightly. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onions and tomatoes are not part of this recipe; the masala here depends on proper blooming of the seeds and spices, then enough simmering for the vegetables to soften evenly.
- If the pan looks dry before the potatoes are cooked, add a splash more hot water. The finished dish should be lightly sauced, not watery.
- Keep the cauliflower in reasonably even florets so it cooks at the same pace as the potatoes.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat best over low heat with a little water stirred in.
- The flavour settles after resting, so this is a good dish to make ahead for the next day.
