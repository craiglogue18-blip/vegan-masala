## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon oil (any neutral oil)
- 2 tablespoons chana dal (20 grams, husked and split Bengal gram)
- ½ cup peanuts (80 grams, raw)
- 12 to 15 curry leaves (small to medium-sized)
- 2 to 3 green chilies (or dry red chilies)
- 2 garlic cloves (small to medium-sized)
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing, optional)
- 1 tablespoon white sesame seeds (10 grams)
- salt (to taste)
- ¾ cup water (or as needed)

## Method
1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a pan over low heat and add 2 tablespoons chana dal.
2. Stir the chana dal often and toast until it turns deep golden, taking care not to let it burn.
3. Remove the chana dal from the pan and set it aside.
4. In the same pan, add ½ cup peanuts and roast over low to medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes, until crisp and lightly browned.
5. Add 12 to 15 curry leaves, 2 to 3 green chilies, 2 garlic cloves, and 1 pinch asafoetida, then sauté for about 1 minute until the curry leaves turn crisp.
6. Stir in 1 tablespoon white sesame seeds and turn off the heat.
7. Let the mixture cool to room temperature, then transfer it to a blender along with the roasted chana dal and salt to taste.
8. Add ¾ cup water and blend until smooth, adding a little more water only if needed for the texture you like.
9. Transfer the peanut chutney to a bowl and serve.

## Notes
- For a thicker chutney, keep the water at the lower end; for a looser version, add a little more after blending.
- The chutney will thicken slightly as it rests, so loosen it with a spoonful of water before serving if needed.
- Store in the refrigerator in an airtight container for 2 to 3 days. Stir well before serving, since the peanut oil can settle.
- Use dry red chilies instead of green chilies if you want a deeper, sharper heat.
- This works well with idli, dosa, vada, or as a sandwich spread.
