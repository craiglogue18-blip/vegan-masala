## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Boil 3 medium potatoes until tender, then drain, cool slightly and cut into cubes.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, 1 green chilli, finely chopped, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 4 cloves garlic, minced. Sauté until the onion turns golden and the raw smell softens.
4. Stir in 2 tsp coriander powder and 1/2 tsp turmeric powder. Cook for a minute so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add the potato cubes and sprinkle over 1 tsp salt. Toss gently so the potatoes are well coated without breaking up.
6. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring now and then, until the masala clings to the potatoes and the edges look slightly dry.
7. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve warm.

## Notes
- Use boiled potatoes that hold their shape; very soft potatoes will break apart in the pan. A waxier variety gives the cleanest finish.
- If the masala starts sticking before the potatoes are coated, lower the heat and add a small splash of water. Let it cook off before stirring again.
- This keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in a pan so the potatoes dry out a little and the cumin aroma returns.
- If you want more heat, add another chilli with the onion rather than at the end. That gives the dish a more even warmth.
