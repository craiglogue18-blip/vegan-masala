## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 200 g spinach
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until golden at the edges and softened through.
3. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the raw smell settles.
4. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, along with 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1 tsp chilli powder. Cook until the tomato breaks down and the masala looks thick and glossy.
5. Add 3 medium potatoes, cubed, and turn them in the masala until well coated.
6. Pour in 1 cup water, cover, and cook on a low simmer until the potatoes are tender and most of the water has reduced.
7. Fold in 200 g spinach and cook until wilted and evenly mixed through.
8. Season with 1 tsp salt, stir well, and cook for another minute so the seasoning settles into the potatoes.

## Notes
- Let the onions take on a little colour before adding the spices; that gives the masala more body and keeps the dish from tasting flat.
- If the pan looks dry while the tomato is cooking, add a splash of water rather than letting the spices catch on the base.
- Cook the potatoes until they are tender right through but still hold their shape. They should take on the masala, not turn to mash.
- The spinach only needs a short cooking time. Add it at the end so it stays green and folds cleanly into the potatoes.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a spoonful of water to loosen the masala.
- This dish thickens as it sits, so a little extra water may be needed when reheating.
