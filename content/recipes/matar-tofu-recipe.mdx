## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 150 g green peas, fresh or frozen
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, divided

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and golden at the edges.
3. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 minute, until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down and the masala thickens.
5. Mix in 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tbsp of the fresh coriander. Cook for a minute.
6. Add 150 g green peas and 200 g firm tofu, cubed, then pour in 1 cup water.
7. Simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until the curry has body and the tofu is warmed through.
8. Stir in 1 tsp garam masala and finish with the remaining 1 tbsp fresh coriander.
9. Serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion and tomato base properly; that is where the body of the curry comes from.
- Frozen peas can go straight in. If using fresh peas, simmer a few minutes longer until tender.
- Press the tofu if it holds a lot of water; it will stay firmer in the curry.
- Taste at the end and adjust salt or chilli after the masala has finished cooking.
- Leftovers keep well for 3 days in the fridge and reheat gently with a splash of water if the curry has thickened too much.
