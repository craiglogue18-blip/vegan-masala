## Ingredients
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 2 tablespoons oil (coconut or vegetable)
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, bell peppers, etc.)
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cups water
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnishing

## Method
1. Rinse 1 cup basmati rice until the water runs clear. Soak it in fresh water for 15 minutes, then drain well.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a pot, pressure cooker, or Instant Pot on medium heat. Add 1 large thinly sliced onion and cook until deep golden at the edges.
3. Add 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds and 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds. Let them crackle, then stir in 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell fades.
4. Add 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder and 1 teaspoon garam masala. Stir for about 30 seconds so the spices bloom in the hot oil.
5. Add 1 cup mixed vegetables and 2 medium chopped tomatoes. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the masala thickens slightly and the vegetables are coated.
6. Stir in the drained rice, then add 2 cups water and salt to taste. Mix gently so the grains stay intact.
7. If using an Instant Pot, cook on high pressure for 5 minutes and let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes before opening. In a pressure cooker, cook for 2 whistles, then rest off the heat. In a pot, cover and cook on low until the rice is done and the water is absorbed.
8. Rest the biryani for 5 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork. Finish with fresh coriander leaves.

## Notes
- Soaking the rice helps the grains cook evenly and stay separate. Drain it well before it goes into the pot.
- Keep the onion a deep golden colour, not pale. That colour gives the biryani its base and helps the masala taste rounded.
- If your vegetables are soft-cooking, add them closer to the rice stage so they do not turn mushy.
- For leftovers, cool the biryani quickly and refrigerate in a covered container for up to 3 days. Reheat with a spoonful of water over low heat.
- A small bowl of unsweetened vegan yogurt or cucumber salad works well if you want something cool alongside it.
