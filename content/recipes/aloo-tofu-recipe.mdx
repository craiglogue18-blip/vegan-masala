## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil, divided
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup water
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- lemon wedge (optional)

## Method
1. Pat 200 g cubed firm tofu dry with a clean towel, then pan-fry it in 1 tbsp of the oil until lightly golden. Set it aside.
2. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil in a pan and add 1 tsp cumin seeds. Let them sizzle for a few seconds.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped and cook until it turns deep golden and sweet at the edges.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for a minute until the raw smell fades.
5. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped and cook them down until they soften and the masala starts to leave the sides of the pan.
6. Sprinkle in 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, and 1 tsp salt. Stir well so the spices cook in the oil rather than clumping.
7. Add 3 medium potatoes, cubed and 1 cup water. Cover and simmer until the potatoes are tender and the gravy has thickened.
8. Fold in the tofu and simmer gently for a few minutes so it takes on the masala without breaking up.
9. Finish with 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp sugar (optional). Taste and adjust salt if needed.
10. Stir through 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped and serve with a squeeze from a lemon wedge, if using.

## Notes
- Cook the onion properly; it gives the masala its base. If it stays pale, the curry will taste flat.
- The potatoes should be soft but still hold their shape. If they are undercooked, leave the lid on a little longer and add a splash of water only if the pan looks dry.
- Tofu works best when it is browned first. That short pan-fry keeps it firm and helps it hold its shape in the curry.
- If the masala starts sticking before the tomatoes have broken down, lower the heat and add a spoonful of water rather than letting it scorch.
- This keeps well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the hob with a small splash of water to loosen the gravy.
- The flavour settles after resting, so leftovers often taste even better the next day.
