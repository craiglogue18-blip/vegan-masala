## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- a pinch of asafoetida (optional)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 200g dried black chickpeas, soaked overnight, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Soak 200g dried black chickpeas overnight, then drain and rinse well.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pressure cooker or heavy pot over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds and let them crackle.
3. Add a pinch of asafoetida, if using, then 1 large onion, finely chopped. Cook slowly until the onion turns light golden at the edges.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 1 minute, until the raw smell softens.
5. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down and the masala looks thick and glossy.
6. Mix in 1 tsp chilli powder, 2 tsp coriander powder, and 1/2 tsp turmeric powder. Cook for 30 seconds so the spices bloom in the oil.
7. Add the soaked chickpeas, 1 cup water, and 1 tsp salt. Stir well, then cover and pressure cook for 10 to 12 whistles, or simmer covered in a pot until the chickpeas are fully tender.
8. Once cooked, stir in 1 tsp garam masala and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes. If the curry feels too loose, mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken it.
9. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- The onion and tomato base should be cooked properly; if they stay sharp, the curry will taste flat even after the chickpeas are done.
- Kala chana should be tender but still hold its shape. If you are simmering instead of pressure cooking, add a little more water as needed.
- For a thicker curry, mash some chickpeas into the masala at the end rather than reducing the liquid too far.
- Leftovers keep well for 3 days in the fridge and usually taste fuller the next day. Reheat with a splash of water so the sauce loosens evenly.
