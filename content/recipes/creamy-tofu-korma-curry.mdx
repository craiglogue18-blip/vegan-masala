## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup vegetable stock
- 1 cup water
- 50 g cashews
- 1 tsp salt
- pepper to taste
- 1 tin coconut milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 2 green cardamom pods and 1/2 tsp cinnamon, and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook slowly until soft and lightly browned at the edges.
3. Stir in 3 garlic cloves, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated. Cook for 1 minute, then add 1 medium tomato, chopped, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chilli powder.
4. Cook the masala down until the tomato breaks up and the mixture looks thick and glossy.
5. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed, and toss gently so the cubes are coated in the masala.
6. Pour in 1 cup vegetable stock, 1 cup water and 1 tsp salt. Bring to a gentle simmer, then cook for 8 to 10 minutes.
7. Blend 50 g cashews with a little of the hot sauce until smooth, then stir the cashew cream back into the pan.
8. Add pepper to taste and 1 tin coconut milk. Simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes, without boiling hard.
9. Turn off the heat and stir in 1 tbsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Check the seasoning and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion properly; a pale, underdone base will leave the curry flat. The masala should smell rounded and look thick before you add the liquids.
- Keep the simmer gentle once the coconut milk goes in. A hard boil can split the sauce and dull the texture.
- If the curry thickens too much on standing, loosen it with a splash of hot water or stock when reheating.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat slowly so the tofu stays tender and the sauce stays smooth.
- Serve with rice or flatbread and finish with a little extra coriander if you like a fresher edge.
