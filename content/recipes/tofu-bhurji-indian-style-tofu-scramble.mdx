## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/2 cup onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon green chilli, chopped
- 1/2 cup bell pepper, chopped
- 1/4 cup tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds, crushed (optional)
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin and coriander powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala (or cinnamon powder)
- 400 grams tofu, drained and pressed, crumbled
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- salt to taste

## Method
1. In a pan, heat 2 tablespoons of oil over medium heat and add 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds.
2. Once the seeds sizzle, add 1/2 cup of chopped onion, 1 tablespoon of minced ginger-garlic, and 1 tablespoon of chopped green chilli.
3. Sauté for about a minute, then add 1/2 cup of chopped bell pepper.
4. When the bell pepper begins to soften, stir in 1/4 cup of chopped tomatoes, 1/2 teaspoon of crushed fennel seeds (if using), and the spice powders: 1 teaspoon of red chilli powder, 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder, and 1 teaspoon of ground cumin and coriander.
5. Add the crumbled tofu (400 grams) and mix well, then season with salt to taste.
6. Cook for another 3-4 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld, and finish with 1 tablespoon of lemon juice.
7. Optionally, garnish with fresh chopped cilantro.

## Notes
- For a creamier texture, consider adding a small splash of plant-based cream or milk during cooking.
- Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheated gently on the stovetop. The dish holds its flavor well, but avoid overcooking.
- Serve warm with avocado toast or alongside roti for a fulfilling breakfast or quick lunch.
