## Ingredients
- 400 g onions, finely sliced
- 750 ml neutral oil, for deep-frying
- 100 g gram flour (besan)
- 30 g rice flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 150 ml water

## Method
1. Put the 400 g finely sliced onions in a mixing bowl.
2. Add 100 g gram flour, 30 g rice flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper, 1/2 tsp ground cumin, 1/2 tsp ground coriander, and 1/2 tsp sea salt.
3. Mix well, then pour in 150 ml water little by little until the onions are coated in a thick batter that holds together.
4. Heat 750 ml neutral oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat until hot enough for a small piece of batter to sizzle at once.
5. Drop spoonfuls of the mixture into the oil and fry in batches without crowding the pan.
6. Turn the bhajis once the underside is deep golden, then fry until crisp and evenly browned.
7. Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper.
8. Serve straight away while the edges are still crisp.

## Notes
- The batter should cling to the onions rather than run off them; add the water gradually so you stop at a thick, rough coating.
- Keep the oil hot between batches. If it drops too far, the bhajis will absorb oil instead of crisping.
- These are best eaten fresh from the pan. If you have leftovers, reheat them in a hot oven or air fryer so the outside firms up again.
- Store in the fridge in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
