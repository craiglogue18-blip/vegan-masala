## Ingredients
- 250g/9oz roasted unsalted peanuts
- 100g/3½oz dates, roughly chopped
- 100g/3½oz chia seeds, divided
- 50g/1¾oz linseeds (also known as flaxseeds), divided
- 50g/1¾oz desiccated coconut
- 2 tbsp warm water, plus more if needed

## Method
1. Put 250g roasted unsalted peanuts, 100g roughly chopped dates, 50g chia seeds and 25g linseeds into a food processor. Blitz for 2–3 minutes, scraping down once or twice, until the mixture starts to gather and look coarse and sticky.
2. Add 2 tbsp warm water and blend for about 1 minute more, until the mixture holds together when pressed between your fingers. If it still feels crumbly, add a little more warm water, 1 teaspoon at a time.
3. Tip the mixture onto a board and divide it into 20 equal portions. Roll each portion firmly into a ball.
4. Mix 50g desiccated coconut with the remaining 50g chia seeds and 25g linseeds in a shallow bowl. Roll each ball in the coating until evenly covered.

## Notes
- The texture should be firm and chewy, not oily or pasty. If the dates are dry, soak them in hot water for 5 minutes, drain well and continue.
- These keep well in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week. They also freeze well; thaw in the fridge before serving.
- Roll the balls firmly so they hold their shape. If the mixture softens while rolling, chill it for 10 minutes and continue.
- For a cleaner finish, pulse the coating lightly so the seeds sit more evenly on the outside.
