## Ingredients
- 1 cup gram flour (besan)
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 cup spinach, chopped
- 1 large potato, thinly sliced
- 1 cup cauliflower florets
- 1 cup water
- 750 ml neutral oil, for deep-frying

## Method
1. In a mixing bowl, combine 1 cup gram flour (besan), 1 teaspoon cumin seeds, 1 teaspoon garam masala, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder, and 1 tsp salt.
2. Add 1 large onion, thinly sliced, 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped, 1 cup spinach, chopped, 1 large potato, thinly sliced, and 1 cup cauliflower florets.
3. Pour in 1 cup water a little at a time and mix until the vegetables are coated in a thick batter that clings without dripping.
4. Heat 750 ml neutral oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
5. Drop spoonfuls of the batter-coated vegetables into the hot oil, keeping the pieces small so they cook through evenly.
6. Fry for 5-7 minutes, turning as needed, until the pakora are deep golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels and serve at once.

## Notes
- The batter should be thick enough to coat the vegetables lightly; if it turns loose, the pakora will absorb too much oil.
- Slice the onion and potato thinly so they cook through in the frying time. The cauliflower florets should be small enough to soften before the outside overcolours.
- Let the oil come fully up to temperature before frying. If it is too cool, the pakora will spread and feel greasy.
- These are at their best straight from the pan. Reheat in a hot oven or air fryer until crisp again, rather than microwaving.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
