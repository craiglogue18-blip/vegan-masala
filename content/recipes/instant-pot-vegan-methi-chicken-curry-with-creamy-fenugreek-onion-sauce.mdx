## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup dry soy curls
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)
- 1 cup plant milk
- 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté mode and heat 2 tbsp oil.
2. Add 2 bay leaves, 2 green cardamom pods, 4 cloves and 1 small cinnamon stick. Let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges.
4. Stir in 4 cloves garlic, minced, 1 inch ginger, grated, and 1 green chilli, finely chopped. Cook for 30 to 45 seconds.
5. Add 1 medium tomato, chopped, and cook until it breaks down and the masala looks pulpy.
6. Blend 1 cup water with the onion-tomato mixture in the pot, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin and 1 tsp salt until smooth.
7. Stir in 1 cup dry soy curls, 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt and 1 tsp sugar, if using. Mix so the soy curls are coated in the masala.
8. Seal the pot and pressure cook for 15 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally.
9. Open the lid and stir in 1 cup plant milk. Simmer on sauté mode until the curry looks creamy and slightly thickened.
10. Add 2 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves and 1 tsp garam masala. Stir through and finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
11. Taste and adjust salt before serving.

## Notes
- Take time with the onion base; that stage gives the curry body and keeps the sauce from tasting flat.
- Plant yoghurt can split if the heat is too high, so stir it in before pressure cooking and keep the pot well mixed.
- If the curry looks loose after the pressure cook, simmer a few minutes longer with the lid open so the sauce reduces properly.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and the methi flavour settles nicely overnight.
- Reheat gently with a splash of water or plant milk so the soy curls stay tender rather than dry.
