## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder (or to taste)
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp salt, or to taste
- 120 ml water, plus more if needed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped and divided

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add 1 finely chopped onion and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring often, until soft, golden and lightly sweet.
2. Add 3 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 tbsp grated ginger. Cook for 30 to 45 seconds, just until the raw smell fades.
3. Stir in 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder and 2 tsp garam masala. Fry for 15 to 20 seconds, stirring constantly.
4. Add 1 can chopped tomatoes and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes break down and the masala looks darker and thicker.
5. Add 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed, 1 tsp salt and 120 ml water. Stir well and lightly mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pan to help the sauce thicken.
6. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, until the chickpeas are well coated and the sauce clings to them. Add a splash more water if the pan dries out before the chickpeas are ready.
7. Stir in 1 tbsp lemon juice and most of 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped. Taste and adjust salt or chilli if needed.
8. Scatter over the remaining coriander and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take your time with the onion and tomato base; that steady cooking gives the masala depth and keeps the sauce from tasting sharp.
- For a thicker chana, mash more chickpeas into the sauce near the end of cooking.
- If the curry sits for a few minutes before serving, the sauce will settle and thicken a little more.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days in a sealed container and reheat gently with a splash of water.
- This freezes well for up to 2 months. Thaw in the fridge and warm through on the stove rather than rushing it in the microwave.
