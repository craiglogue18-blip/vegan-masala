## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 4 cloves
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 100 ml oat cream
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- crushed black peppercorn, for garnish
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- mint leaves, for garnish

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add 4 cloves, 2 green cardamom pods and 1 small cinnamon stick. Let them sizzle for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped and cook until deep golden, stirring often so it does not catch.
4. Add 1 inch ginger, grated, 4 cloves garlic, minced and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the raw smell settles.
5. Stir in 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder and 1 tsp salt. Cook briefly so the spices coat the onion base.
6. Add 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt and stir until the masala looks smooth and lightly glossy.
7. Add 200 g firm tofu, cubed and turn gently so the pieces stay intact.
8. Pour in 100 ml oat cream, then add 1 cup water to loosen the gravy to a spoonable consistency.
9. Season with 1/2 tsp black pepper and scatter in crushed black peppercorn, then bring to a gentle simmer.
10. Cover the pan with a lid and foil, lower the heat and cook until the tofu has taken on the masala and the gravy has reduced slightly.
11. Lift the lid, stir in 1 red chilli, finely chopped and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
12. Finish with mint leaves, give it one last stir and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take time with the onion base; a well-cooked onion masala gives the gravy depth and keeps the yoghurt from tasting flat.
- Keep the heat gentle after adding the yoghurt and cream so the sauce stays smooth and does not split.
- If the gravy becomes too thick, loosen it with a splash of hot water. If it looks thin, simmer uncovered for a few minutes before serving.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and reheat best over low heat with a little water added to the pan.
- Firm tofu holds its shape here, but pressing it briefly before cooking will give a cleaner bite and a better finish.
