## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 100g cashews (soaked)
- 1 small cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 large potato, cubed
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 100g green peas
- 1 tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp kasuri methi
- 100 ml oat cream
- 1 tsp garam masala

## Method
1. Steam or sauté the 1 small cauliflower cut into florets, 1 large potato cubed, 2 carrots chopped and 100g green peas until just tender, then set aside.
2. Blend 2 medium tomatoes chopped, 100g soaked cashews, 3 garlic cloves minced and 1 inch ginger grated into a smooth paste.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and add 1 bay leaf; let it sizzle for a few seconds.
4. Add the tomato-cashew paste and cook, stirring often, until it thickens and the oil begins to separate at the edges.
5. Stir in 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chilli powder, and cook for 30 seconds to bloom the spices.
6. Add 1 green chilli finely chopped and 1 cup water, then simmer until the gravy loosens slightly and turns glossy.
7. Add the cooked cauliflower, potato, carrots, peas and 1 tin kidney beans drained and rinsed.
8. Season with 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp sugar, then simmer for 5 to 7 minutes so the vegetables take on the masala.
9. Crush 1 tsp kasuri methi between your palms and add it with 100 ml oat cream.
10. Finish with 1 tsp garam masala, simmer gently for a minute, then serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the tomato-cashew base until it loses its raw edge and starts looking glossy; that gives the curry its body.
- Keep the vegetables just tender before adding them to the gravy so they hold shape in the final simmer.
- Adjust salt and sugar only after the curry has simmered with the vegetables.
- The oat cream goes in at the end to keep the sauce smooth; avoid a hard boil after adding it.
- Leftovers keep well for up to 3 days in the fridge and often taste deeper the next day.
- Reheat slowly with a splash of water if the gravy thickens in the fridge.
