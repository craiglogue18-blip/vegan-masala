## Ingredients
- 14 ounces firm or extra firm tofu, pressed for at least 15 minutes and torn into bite-sized pieces
- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder, for the tofu
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons oil, divided
- 2 small dried red chilies
- 10 curry leaves, fresh, frozen, or dried
- 3/4 cup sliced onion
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 green chili, slit
- 2 to 3 tablespoons sliced green bell pepper
- 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, for the sauce
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 1/2 cup water
- Green onions, cilantro, and lime juice

## Method
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (205°C).
2. Place the pressed tofu in a bowl. Add 1 teaspoon oil and toss gently.
3. Mix 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/8 teaspoon turmeric, 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and 2 tablespoons cornstarch in a small bowl. Sprinkle over the tofu and toss until the pieces are evenly coated.
4. Spread the tofu on a parchment-lined baking sheet in a single layer and bake for 22 to 28 minutes, until crisp at the edges. For stovetop cooking, heat 1 teaspoon of the divided oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the tofu until browned and crisp on most sides, about 5 to 6 minutes total. Remove to a plate.
5. In the same skillet, heat the remaining 1 teaspoon oil over medium-high heat. Add 2 small dried red chilies and 10 curry leaves and stir for a few seconds until fragrant.
6. Add 3/4 cup sliced onion and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring often, until the edges take on some colour. Add a splash of water if the pan looks dry.
7. Stir in 1 slit green chili and 2 to 3 tablespoons sliced green bell pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
8. Add 2 tablespoons ginger garlic paste, 1 1/2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, 1/2 to 1 teaspoon garam masala, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, and 2 tablespoons ketchup. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring through the onions and peppers.
9. Stir in 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 1/2 cup water and simmer until the sauce turns glossy and lightly thickened.
10. Add the tofu and toss to coat. Turn off the heat, then finish with green onions, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime juice.

## Notes
- Oven-baked tofu gives the most even crispness; pan-frying gives a deeper, more browned edge. Both work well.
- Add the tofu only after the sauce has thickened slightly; if it goes in too early, the coating softens.
- Leftovers keep for up to 2 days in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet over medium heat so the tofu firms up again.
- If you want more heat, add extra green chili rather than loading up the sauce with more garam masala.
- The sauce should cling to the tofu without pooling in the pan. If it looks too thick, loosen it with a spoonful of water.
