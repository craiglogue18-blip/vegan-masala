## Ingredients
- 3 tbsp unsweetened plant yoghurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 tbsp oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
- 1 cup water
- 100 ml oat cream
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)

## Method
1. In a bowl, mix 3 tbsp plant yoghurt, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1/2 tsp salt.
2. Add 200 g cubed tofu and 1 diced bell pepper. Toss well and leave to marinate for 1 hour.
3. Bake the marinated tofu and vegetables at 200°C for 15–20 minutes, or pan-fry them until the edges are lightly charred.
4. Blend 1 large finely chopped onion and 2 medium chopped tomatoes to a smooth purée.
5. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan and cook the onion paste until it turns golden and loses its raw smell.
6. Add 4 cloves minced garlic and cook for a few seconds, just until fragrant.
7. Stir in the tomato purée and cook until it thickens and the oil begins to separate from the masala.
8. Add 2 tbsp fresh coriander, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves, and the remaining 1/2 tsp salt. Cook for 1 minute.
9. Add 1 cup water and simmer until the gravy has body and the raw tomato taste has gone.
10. Stir in 100 ml oat cream and 1 tin drained chickpeas, then add the baked tofu tikka pieces and pepper.
11. Simmer gently for 2 to 3 minutes. Finish with 1 tsp sugar if needed, then serve hot.
12. Scatter over a little extra fresh coriander if you like before serving.

## Notes
- Cook the masala until it changes colour and the oil starts to show at the edges; that step gives the gravy its depth.
- Add the yoghurt over low heat and stir gently so it does not split.
- If the gravy thickens too much after standing, loosen it with a splash of water when reheating.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and usually taste more settled the next day.
- For a deeper finish, char the tofu and peppers a little more in the oven or pan before adding them to the sauce.
