## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 4 whole cloves
- 4 green cardamoms
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 200 ml tomato puree
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 4 whole cloves and 4 green cardamoms. Let them sizzle for a few seconds.
2. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped and cook, stirring often, until it turns deep golden and the mixture becomes thick.
3. Add 4 cloves garlic, minced and 1 inch ginger, grated. Stir for 30 seconds, just until the raw smell softens.
4. Stir in 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder and 2 tsp coriander powder. Cook for a minute so the spices bloom in the oil.
5. Add 200 ml tomato puree and 1 tsp salt. Cook until the masala thickens and the oil starts to appear around the edges.
6. Add 200 g mushrooms, sliced and stir well so they are coated in the masala.
7. Pour in 1 cup water, then cover and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, until the mushrooms are tender and the gravy has body.
8. Uncover and cook briefly if the gravy looks too thin. Finish with 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) and 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped.
9. Serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion masala properly; that is where the body of the curry comes from. If the onions stay pale, the gravy will taste flat.
- Mushrooms release their own water, so keep the simmer gentle and reduce uncovered only at the end if you want a thicker sauce.
- Taste after the mushrooms have cooked through, then adjust salt if needed. If your tomato puree is very sharp, a few extra minutes of simmering will soften it.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat slowly so the mushrooms do not turn leathery.
- This curry freezes reasonably well, though the mushrooms soften a little after thawing.
