## Ingredients
- 1.5 litres/2½ pints water
- 100g/3½oz toor dal (yellow split pigeon peas), well rinsed
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 100g/3½oz carrots, cut into 2cm/¾in chunks
- 100g/3½oz pumpkin or butternut squash, peeled and cut into 2cm/¾in chunks
- 100g/3½oz green beans, cut into 2cm/¾in lengths
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1½ tsp ground turmeric
- 1½ tsp sugar
- 1½ tsp salt
- 50ml/2fl oz vegetable oil
- 1 tsp chana dal
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 4 dried Kashmiri chillies with seeds
- handful fresh curry leaves
- 1 tsp asafoetida
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 1½ tsp fenugreek seeds

## Method
1. In a large, deep pan, bring 1.5 litres/2½ pints of water to a boil. Add 100g of toor dal, 1 chopped onion, 100g chopped carrots, 100g chopped pumpkin, 100g green beans, and 1 chopped tomato. Lower the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 30 minutes.
2. Stir in 1½ tsp ground turmeric, 1½ tsp sugar, and 1½ tsp salt, then simmer gently for a further 15 minutes, or until the dal is tender.
3. Meanwhile, prepare the masala by heating 50ml of vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add 1 tsp chana dal, 1 tsp fenugreek seeds, 1 tsp coriander seeds, 4 dried Kashmiri chillies, a handful of fresh curry leaves and 1 tsp asafoetida. Fry for 30 seconds to one minute, or until fragrant. Transfer this mixture to a spice grinder, blend to a paste, and stir it into the simmering vegetables.
4. For the tarka, heat 2 tbsp of vegetable oil in a clean frying pan over medium heat. Add 1 finely chopped onion, 1 tsp black mustard seeds, and 1½ tsp fenugreek seeds. Fry for about 10 minutes until the onion softens and turns golden-brown. Pour this tarka into the sambar and mix well before serving.

## Notes
- Sambar should have a thick, stew-like consistency; adjust water as necessary for your preferred texture.
- Leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator for up to three days and reheated on the stove over low heat.
- Sambar is often more flavorful the next day, making it a great option for meal prep.
- Serve with steamed rice, dosas, or fresh idlis. A side of coconut chutney can complement the dish nicely.
