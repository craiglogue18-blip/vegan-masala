## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 5 cardamom pods
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, chopped into small pieces
- 1 tbsp garlic puree
- 2 tbsp tomato puree
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli
- 2 tbsp madras curry powder
- 1 pack Love Seitan Coarse Shredded Seitan Chyck’n (or similar)
- 400ml water
- small handful fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. In a large frying pan, heat 1 tablespoon of neutral oil over medium heat. Add 1 pack of shredded seitan chyck’n and fry until golden and crispy; then, remove from the pan.
2. In the same pan, add 5 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick, and 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, allowing them to bloom for about 30 seconds.
3. Add the chopped onion and sauté for 5 minutes until softened. Then stir in 1 tablespoon of garlic puree and cook for another minute.
4. Incorporate 2 tablespoons of tomato puree, ½ teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon chilli, and 2 tablespoons of madras curry powder. Stir well to combine and cook the spices for a few minutes, then pour in 400ml of water.
5. Return the crispy seitan to the pan, cover, and let simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Before serving, stir in a small handful of chopped fresh coriander.

## Notes
- Adjust the spiciness by varying the amount of chilli used. The sauce will thicken as it simmers, so feel free to add more water for a looser consistency.
- Store any leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, and reheat gently on the stove.
- The texture of the seitan holds up well, making it a satisfying dish even as leftovers.
