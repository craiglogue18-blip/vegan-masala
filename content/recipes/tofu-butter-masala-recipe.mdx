## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 1 bay leaf (optional)
- 1 small cinnamon stick (optional)
- 3 cloves (optional)
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 5–6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 2 cups tomatoes, chopped
- 15 cashews
- 1 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder or paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 cup water (for blending)
- 2 tbsp vegan butter
- 250–300g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 tsp sugar or jaggery (optional)
- ½ tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)
- 3–4 tbsp plant cream or coconut cream
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Add 2 green cardamom pods, 1 bay leaf if using, 1 small cinnamon stick if using, and 3 cloves if using.
2. Add 1 medium onion, sliced, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges. Stir in 5–6 garlic cloves, minced, and 1 inch ginger, grated, and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add 2 cups tomatoes, chopped, 15 cashews, and 1 tsp salt. Cook until the tomatoes collapse and the masala looks thick and glossy.
4. Stir in 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder or paprika, 1 tsp ground coriander, and 1 tsp garam masala. Cook briefly until fragrant, then remove from the heat.
5. Blend the cooked masala with 1 cup water until completely smooth.
6. Melt 2 tbsp vegan butter in the same pan. Add the blended sauce and simmer gently until it thickens slightly and loses the raw tomato edge.
7. Add 250–300g firm tofu, cubed, and simmer for 4 to 5 minutes so the tofu warms through without breaking up.
8. Stir in 1 tsp sugar or jaggery if using, ½ tbsp kasuri methi, and 3–4 tbsp plant cream or coconut cream. Cook for 1 minute, then turn off the heat.
9. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Cook the onion-tomato base properly; that is where the body of the curry comes from. If the tomatoes still taste sharp before blending, give them a few more minutes.
- Blend until smooth for a classic butter masala texture. If the sauce feels too thick after simmering, loosen it with a splash of hot water.
- Add the tofu near the end so it stays tender and holds its shape. Simmer gently rather than boiling hard.
- Taste after the cream and kasuri methi go in, then adjust salt or sweetness once the sauce has settled.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat over low heat with a little water, stirring so the sauce stays smooth.
- Leftovers thicken as they sit; this curry usually tastes better the next day after the spices have settled into the sauce.
