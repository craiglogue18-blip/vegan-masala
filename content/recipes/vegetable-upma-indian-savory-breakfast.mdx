## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon vegan ghee or neutral oil
- 1/4 teaspoon black mustard seeds
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon split urad dal (optional)
- 1 teaspoon chana dal (optional)
- 1/2 cup onions (finely chopped)
- 1 teaspoon ginger (finely chopped)
- 1/2 teaspoon green chili or jalapeño (finely chopped)
- 4-6 curry leaves (optional)
- 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, zucchini, green beans, frozen green peas, etc.)
- 1 3/4 cup water
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup roasted wheat semolina
- 1/4 cup cilantro (chopped)
- 1/2 lime or lemon (freshly squeezed)

## Method
1. Place a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add 1 tablespoon of vegan ghee or neutral oil, followed by 1/4 teaspoon black mustard seeds and 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds. If using, add 1 teaspoon split urad dal and 1 teaspoon chana dal. Stir continuously until the mustard seeds begin to pop and the dal is golden brown.
2. Add 1/2 cup finely chopped onions, 1 teaspoon finely chopped ginger, 1/2 teaspoon finely chopped green chili, and the optional curry leaves. Sauté for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until the onions are lightly browned.
3. Introduce 2 cups of mixed vegetables, stirring well. Add a few tablespoons of water, cover the pan, and cook for 5-10 minutes, until the vegetables start to soften.
4. Pour in 1 3/4 cups of water, adding 1/2 teaspoon salt to season. Raise the heat to bring it to a boil. Once boiling, reduce to low heat, and gradually stir in 1 cup of roasted wheat semolina to prevent lumps. A wooden spoon works best for this.
5. Cover and cook for an additional 5 minutes, allowing the water to be fully absorbed. Turn off the heat and let it sit, covered, for another 2 minutes.
6. Stir in 1/4 cup chopped cilantro and the juice of 1/2 lime or lemon. The upma should have a light and fluffy texture. Taste, and adjust with more salt, lime juice, or cilantro as desired.

## Notes
- For a heartier texture, add more vegetables or adjust the semolina to water ratio slightly. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently with a splash of water to restore moisture.
- When serving, you may top it with additional cilantro or lime for extra brightness.
