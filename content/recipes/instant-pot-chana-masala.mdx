## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp ajwain (optional)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 2 green cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green chillies, finely chopped
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté mode and heat 2 tbsp oil.
2. Add 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp ajwain (if using), 1 bay leaf, 1 small cinnamon stick, 2 green cardamom pods and 4 cloves. Stir for a few seconds until fragrant.
3. Add 1 large onion, finely chopped, and cook until soft and lightly golden at the edges.
4. Stir in 1 inch ginger, grated, 4 cloves garlic, minced, and 2 green chillies, finely chopped. Cook for 30 to 40 seconds.
5. Add 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, and cook until they break down and the masala looks thick and pulpy.
6. Stir in 1 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp chilli powder and cook for a minute so the spices lose their raw edge.
7. Add 1 tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed, 1 tsp salt and 1 cup water. Scrape the bottom of the pot well so nothing is stuck.
8. Close the lid and pressure cook on high for 5 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes before releasing the remainder.
9. Open the lid and switch back to sauté mode. Simmer the chana masala until the gravy thickens to your liking.
10. Mash a few chickpeas against the side of the pot to give the sauce more body, if you want a looser or thinner gravy skip this step.
11. Stir in 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
12. Finish with 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped, and serve hot.

## Notes
- Take the onion and tomato stage far enough for the masala to lose its sharpness; that is where the depth comes from.
- For a thicker gravy, mash a few chickpeas during the final simmer. For a looser sauce, add a splash more hot water.
- If the gravy tastes flat after pressure cooking, let it simmer a few minutes longer before adding the lemon juice.
- Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to 3 days and the spice base settles nicely overnight.
- Reheat gently on the stove or in the microwave, adding a little water if the sauce has thickened too much.
- Serve with something plain alongside so the chana can carry the meal.
