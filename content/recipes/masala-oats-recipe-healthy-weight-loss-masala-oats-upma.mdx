## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegan ghee or neutral oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 pods cardamom
- ½ tsp cloves
- 1 inch cinnamon stick
- ½ onion (finely chopped)
- 2 green chillies (slit)
- ½ carrot (chopped)
- 5 green beans (chopped)
- 2 tbsp peas
- ½ tomato (chopped)
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp pav bhaji masala
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 cup palak (chopped)
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves (chopped)

## Method
1. In a large kadai, heat 2 tbsp vegan ghee or neutral oil over medium heat. Add 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 4 pods cardamom, ½ tsp cloves, and 1 inch cinnamon stick, sautéing until the spices release their aroma.
2. Add ½ onion and 2 green chillies, cooking until the onion becomes soft and translucent.
3. Incorporate ½ carrot, 5 green beans, 2 tbsp peas, and ½ tomato, stirring for about a minute until the tomato softens.
4. Add ½ tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp pav bhaji masala, and ½ tsp salt, sautéing until fragrant.
5. Stir in 1 cup palak and cook for 2 minutes until wilted.
6. Pour in 3 cups water, bringing the mixture to a rolling boil.
7. Once boiling, add 1 cup rolled oats, stirring well to combine.
8. Cover and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, adjusting with additional water as needed, until the oats are tender.
9. Finally, mix in 2 tsp lemon juice and 2 tbsp chopped coriander before serving.

## Notes
- This dish has a creamy texture from the cooked oats and is full of vibrant vegetables. Store any leftovers in an airtight container for up to 2 days, reheating gently on the stove with a splash of water if needed.
- Feel free to adjust the spice levels by adding more or less chilli, according to your preference.
